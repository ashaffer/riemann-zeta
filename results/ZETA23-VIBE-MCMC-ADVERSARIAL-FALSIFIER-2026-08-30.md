# Adversarial kernel for a stochastic uniform-strip proof search

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** proof-search protocol and hostile candidate audit; no zero-free
strip and no proof of RH

## 0. Outcome

The useful interpretation of a latent "vibe-proof" sampler is not voting
among persuasive narratives.  It is proposal generation for typed theorem
DAGs, followed by immediate rejection whenever an edge changes a quantifier,
scale, mask, normalization, sign, or dependence.

Thirty-three common proposal motifs were screened below.  Twenty-seven collapse
against existing exact countermodels or quantifier audits.  Six survive only
as explicit new theorem cards.  The hostile pass found one additional error
one level above SR2PF: a general Fejer autocorrelation can be generated by an
arbitrary sparse set, not just a digit product.  A sparse Sidon-like generator
has participation about `m^2`, remains dispersed, and need contain neither a
long progression nor a large Cartesian tensor.  The present hypotheses give
only a *lower* participation bound.  They do not force the low entropy needed
to enter tensor-labelled SR2PF.

Accordingly, the highest-information survivor in the current QP program is
an **event-conditioned participation-stability theorem**, followed by a
source-preserving actual-prime inverse theorem.  Only after stability has
forced a low-entropy/high-additive-energy state is it credible to extract
either a hereditary localized carrier, a same-side pointwise low-rank packet,
or an all-cut digit tensor.  The first two outputs have branch terminators and
the third is now excluded by tensor-labelled SR2PF.

This would prove the LTRAD input, not the strip.  Prime-supported DPA remains
independently open unless the inverse theorem is coupled to an
event-conditioned upper certificate.  A sample which forgets that second
input is rejected.

## 1. The sampler's state space

A proof state is a directed acyclic graph.  Every node carries the tuple

```text
(claim, quantifiers, scale, node mask, normalization,
 sign/order relation, allowed dependence, inherited data).
```

Every proposed edge must be one of:

```text
identity / imported theorem / proved local lemma / explicitly open lemma.
```

A prose analogy is not an edge.  Numerical evidence may propose an edge but
cannot certify it.  The energy assigned to a state is

```text
E = infinity,  if any hard type check fails;
E = sum complexity(open lemmas) + circularity penalty otherwise.
```

Two states collapse to the same state if their remaining open lemma is
equivalent, by an audited reduction, to a fixed strip.  This prevents a
coordinate change from being scored as progress.

Suggested proposal moves are dualize, condition on a hypothetical zero,
localize, tensorize, random-restrict, amplify, complete, take a coboundary,
switch arithmetic families, or replace a universal assertion by an
event-conditioned assertion.  After each move run the tests in Sections 2--6.

## 2. Nonnegotiable quantifier tests

`Q1` **Strip target.**  The output must give one fixed `delta>0` and exclude
every zeta zero with `Re(rho)>1-delta`.  A height-dependent delta, a sequence
of heights, density one, or almost every height does not pass.

`Q2` **All scales.**  An arithmetic bound used through the fixed-window
reduction must hold for every sufficiently large real `x`.  Dyadic,
subsequential, averaged, or almost-all-`x` estimates require a separately
proved interpolation theorem.

`Q3` **Parameters freeze first.**  In the audited fixed-window theorem,
`ell,k,eta,theta` are fixed before `x` tends to infinity.  A moving `k(x)`
needs a new moving-test converse and uniform control of constants, currently
as large as `exp(O(k log k))` at fixed total width.

`Q4` **Per-zero is allowed, vacuity is not evidence.**  A domain and a
positive saving may depend on a selected hypothetical zero.  But a
zero-conditioned estimate false at every candidate point is merely a
reformulation of zero absence, not an independently proved lemma.

`Q5` **One zero defeats averages.**  Zero-density, moment, or probabilistic
statements alone do not exclude an isolated off-strip zero.  They pass only
with a proved replication or pointwise amplification mechanism.

`Q6` **Uniform constants survive limits.**  Every `o(1)` used to beat a fixed
exponent gap must be uniform over the selected nodes, events, and extracted
subsets.

## 3. Exponent and integrality tests

At the current digit gate,

```text
B=Y^(50/33),
frequency tolerance = B^-1=Y^(-50/33),
physical prime tolerance = Y/B=Y^(-17/33),
minor height H=Y^2/B=Y^(16/33).
```

`E1` **Do not confuse the two errors.**  Rounding a synthetic physical node
to an integer gives logarithmic error `O(Y^-1)`, larger than `B^-1` by
`Y^(17/33)`.  It cannot preserve the separator floor.

`E2` **Correct determinant degree.**  A `3 x 3` flattening determinant is
`O(Y(Y/B)^2)=Y^(-1/33)` because the zero- and one-error terms vanish; hence it
is exactly zero.  A `2 x 2` minor is only `O(H)=Y^(16/33)` and is generally a
nonzero integer.

`E3` **Directional ledger.**  The benchmark chain needs

```text
c_DPA=.019 > c_rad=.0189,
d_dir=.001,
c_transverse=c_rad-d_dir=.0179.
```

With Turan aperture `A'=1`, the eventual strip width is `10^-6`.  Reversing
an inequality between `Y^-c` terms is an automatic rejection.

`E4` **Four-cycle conversion.**  The existing generic conversion of even a
sharp four-cycle input gives only `Y^(-41/66-o(1))`, not the required
`Y^(-.0179+o(1))`.  The missing exponent is about `.6033`; it cannot be
called an epsilon loss.

`E5` **Fixed power, not subpower.**  A Vinogradov--Korobov-shaped error
`x^(1/2) exp(-c(log x)^(3/5)(loglog x)^(-1/5))` has effective saving tending
to zero.  It does not imply a fixed strip.

`E6` **Integer floors remain visible.**  `Y=N+1/2` is used in calibrated
antipodes; `U=V=floor(x^theta)` is used in Vaughan.  A scale coboundary must
freeze `U,V` on a block and account for cores and endpoints.  Differencing a
globally moving cutoff as if fixed fails.

`E7` **Source phase is not tensor accuracy.**  A source height
`t_0<=Y` resolves a log frequency only to `1/t_0>=Y^-1`.  SR2PF needs
`B^-1=Y^(-50/33)`, finer by at least `Y^(17/33)`.  Any inverse extraction
must obtain this superresolution from the full high-band separator/contact
conditions, not from rounding the source phases.

## 4. Mask, carrier, and sign inheritance tests

`M1` **Actual primes.**  Extracted nodes must be distinct ordinary primes in
the required fixed shell and on the required side.  Synthetic real nodes,
integers, prime powers, or affine prime fixtures do not silently transfer.

`M2` **Signed deletion is unsafe.**  Proper powers can be deleted from the
positive strip-to-DPA weights at cost `Y^(-1/2+o(1))`.  They cannot be
deleted from an arbitrary signed separator without a coefficient bound.

`M3` **Carrier normalization.**  Preserve both the relevant normalization
(`sum y=1` when required) and the source constraint `y dot v=-1`.  A local
piece with large energy or source participation does not inherit either.

`M4` **Hereditary floor.**  To invoke localized-carrier exclusion, the
complete retained carrier needs its own one-sided floor.  The complement may
cancel the local piece at every high-band height.  Rescaling a local island
does not fix this.

`M5` **Separator cap.**  In a B3 contradiction the premise
`h_Y(y)<=Y^(-.0179+o(1))` cannot be replaced by the corrector-energy bound
derived from it.  The one-pair null corrector is a concrete failure
certificate.

`M6` **Generic BSG loses the payload.**  Energy extraction does not preserve
coefficient signs, source vector, floor, prime mask, carrier identity, or
weighted `B^-1` accuracy.  An AP in an index set is not automatically a
same-side pointwise log progression.

`M7` **All cuts.**  Tensor-labelled SR2PF uses rank at most two under every
bipartition.  One rank-two matrix flattening is too broad; additive prime
grids and explicit rank-two prime matrices survive.

`S1` **No invented positivity.**  Actual-prime reflection is unbounded above
and below.  Finite Type-II audits also exhibit both signs and near-total
diagonal cancellation.

`S2` **Complete before estimating.**  The Vaughan observable retains all
unequal-product terms, the tail--center cross term, and the center square.
Fixed cofactor blocks and squarefree parity sectors have worse poles
separately; only their completed sum has the required simple-pole structure.

`S3` **Continuous band.**  A finite time grid certifies a continuum floor only
with a derivative guard depending on the actual coefficient `L1` norm.  A
large signed norm can invalidate an apparently fine grid.

### 4.1 General Fejer stress family

The digit box is not the general abstract obstruction.  Let `A` be any set
of `m` distinct integers, let

```text
nu(d)=#{(a,b) in A^2:a-b=d},
K(t)=m^-1 |sum_(a in A) exp(i omega a t)|^2,
y_d=-nu(d)/[m(m-1)]                    (d>0).
```

Then identically

```text
F_y(t)=[1-K(t)]/[2(m-1)] <=1/[2(m-1)].
```

At every common period, `F_y=-1/2` and
`y dot (a(t_0)+q_0)=-1`.  Thus the same source/floor mechanism exists for
arbitrary generators.  If `A` is a sufficiently sparse random or Sidon-like
subset of `[0,R]`, its positive difference support is physically dispersed
after the usual `u_d=omega d` embedding, while

```text
sum_d nu(d)^2 asyp m^2,
P_eff asyp m^2.
```

For scale intuition, one may take `m=Y^.0179` and
`R=m^10=Y^.179`.  Standard sparse-random counting makes nontrivial repeated
differences and four-term progressions in the positive difference set
avoidable (even a degenerate eight-variable one-equation count is bounded at
the scale `m^8/R=o(1)`).  Then physical gaps are at least
`Y/R=Y^.821`, while any Cartesian digit box
with four states in a mode would contain a forbidden four-term progression.
This probabilistic stress construction should be formalized before being
used as a published counterexample, but the exact Fejer identity itself is
unconditional and already exposes the missing hypothesis.

A small deterministic fixture is

```text
A={291,1037,1093,1796,2003,2459,3193,4239}.
```

Its 28 positive differences are all distinct and contain no four-term
arithmetic progression.  It is only a finite sanity check, not the required
asymptotic family.

In contrast, digit boxes have much larger additive energy and
`P_eff=Y^(.0179+o(1))`.  The already-proved estimate
`P_eff>>Y^.0179` allows both regimes.  Consequently an inverse theorem which
lists only localization, a pointwise progression, and the SR2PF tensor is
false at the abstract level unless it assumes or proves participation
stability/low entropy.  Actual-prime arithmetic might rule out the sparse
family, but that would itself be a new theorem and cannot be inferred from
SR2PF.

### 4.2 Diffuse Bessel-saturation stress family

Near equality in the corrector square-function estimate does not by linear
algebra imply low rank.  Let `Delta` carrier-positive queries be orthogonal,
let their normalized evaluation map be a coisometry `U`, and let the carrier
values form a vector `c` with every coordinate of size `epsilon`.  The
corrector

```text
z=-U^*c
```

cancels all queries and has exactly

```text
||z||_2=||c||_2=epsilon sqrt(Delta).
```

This saturates the proved budget while the evaluation matrix can have stable
rank `Delta` and no peelable tensor factors.  With an ambient dimension
larger than `Delta`, one source-null linear condition can be imposed by a
negligible perturbation.  Random Fourier matrices supply the corresponding
synthetic phase model.

Therefore the proposed dichotomy

```text
strict Bessel deficit / tensor-structured equality
```

omits a generic third branch: diffuse isometric equality.  Actual-prime
labels or the one-sided continuum cap might exclude that branch, but such an
exclusion is precisely a new arithmetic theorem; Bessel stability alone does
not.

## 5. Topological and analytic-access tests

`T1` **Rouche winding.**  Ordinary and hybrid universality allow
nonvanishing targets.  Such a target cannot approximate a boundary enclosing
a zeta zero inside the Rouche threshold.  Finite Euler products have the
same wrong-winding defect.

`T2` **Replication before density.**  To use a density bound, prove a lower
bound for the measure or number of translated copies of the selected zero.
Finite prime-phase recurrence reproduces detector values, not the divisor.

`T3` **Fixed contour.**  For the actual finite Euler head the R176 fixed
contour integral has the wrong main term for `q=1` and grows at least
`(q-1)H^epsilon/(log H)^2` on a transverse left arc for `q>=2`.

`T4` **Moving contour access.**  A bounded phase-following corridor has right
harmonic measure `O(1/log H)`.  A useful contour must accept critical growth
and then prove a genuinely arithmetic strict deficit; geometry alone cannot
provide it.

`T5` **Explicit-formula test signs.**  A proposed Weil/Stechkin kernel must
state its Fourier support, the sign of every prime contribution, the sign of
the selected off-axis zero contribution, and bounds for the pole and gamma
terms.  Calling the kernel positive definite is not enough.

## 7. The six surviving theorem cards

### A. Participation stability, then source-preserving inverse extraction

First prove that a genuine *extremal* actual-prime separator arising from a
bad event has the missing upper bound

```text
P_eff<=Y^(.0179+o(1)),
```

or another quantitative low-entropy statement strong enough to exclude the
sparse-generator Fejer family in Section 4.1.  The choice of extremizer and
all constants must be explicit; the known direction is only
`P_eff>>Y^.0179`.

Only under that new stability conclusion, extract from an actual
prime-supported vector satisfying

From an actual prime-supported vector satisfying

```text
y dot v=-1,
h_Y(y)<=Y^(-.0179+o(1)),
```

extract, with uniform constants and retained source mass, one of:

1. a complete carrier in an interval of width below
   `Y^((1+.0179)/3-o(1))`, with its own normalization and floor;
2. a same-side `B^-1`-accurate pointwise log progression longer than
   `C log Y`; or
3. a weighted actual-prime digit box whose cleaned restriction has the
   separated product labels and rank at most two under every bipartition.

Existing branch terminators exclude all three outputs.  The theorem must not
replace output 3 by high energy or by one low-rank flattening.  The two-step
card, not an unconditional trichotomy, is the cleanest current way to turn
the SR2PF work into LTRAD.

### B. Event-conditioned upper/lower coupling

For every genuine long negative source event, prove directly

```text
r_P(H_Y)<=Y^(-.019+o(1))
and
r_P(H_Y)>=Y^(-.0189-o(1)).
```

It is enough that the upper certificate exist at event-carrying centers; a
universal DPA theorem is stronger than necessary.  A promising proposal may
use the same event phases in both dual optimizations, but it must exhibit the
certificate rather than infer it from the lower theorem.

### C. Complete centered Type-II cancellation

For some fixed admissible `ell,k,theta,eta>0`, prove for all sufficiently
large real `x`

```text
|B_(U,V)^(k)(x)-Z_(U,V)^(k)(x)| << x^(1/2-eta),
U=V=floor(x^theta).
```

This passes directly to a fixed strip.  It is not an intermediate
simplification: the fixed-window audit shows it is equivalent, up to an
arbitrarily small exponent loss, to the desired strip.  A new method must see
the completed even/odd squarefree cancellation and moving ramp observable.

### D. Quantitative punctured self-recurrence

For the boundary of a disc around a hypothetical off-strip zero with an arc
of length `ell` removed, prove a recurrence-density lower bound strong enough
to dominate the `O(ell^2)` derivative-fill bad set and the applicable
zero-density exponent.  This avoids the literal nonvanishing-target
contradiction only if the dependence on `ell` is explicit.  "Positive
density depending on the compact set" is insufficient.

### E. Observable-specific spectral gap

Construct a non-diagonal arithmetic metric or transfer operator for which
the **completed ramp observable**, not the ambient Euler evolution, has a
uniform contraction by `Y^-eta`.  It must retain the center and cofactor sum
and survive the known expanding Euler modes.  Uniform coercivity on the
whole state space is already refuted; an observable quotient is essential.

### F. Prime-positive explicit-formula witness

For each hypothetical zero with `beta>1-delta`, construct a legal test whose
zero term has a fixed adverse sign while its complete prime, pole, and gamma
side has the opposite sign.  The construction must be uniform in the
ordinate and cannot use the zero-free conclusion to control the prime side.
This is a precise search target for SDP-assisted kernel discovery, but its
prior is low because unrestricted Weil positivity is RH-equivalent and
classical positive-polynomial arguments lose a height-dependent strip.

## 8. Posterior ordering

By information gain rather than probability of immediate proof:

```text
1. A: tests the newly exposed stability gap before spending effort on tensor
      extraction; either sign identifies the true growing-rank branch.
2. B: attacks the unnecessary universal split and could remove one major
      open input.
3. D: has a sharp quantitative pass/fail threshold and is genuinely
      orthogonal to QP.
4. E: targets the exact completed arithmetic survivor, though the theorem
      design is presently underspecified.
5. F: experimentally accessible but likely to rediscover an equivalent
      positivity criterion.
6. C: logically cleanest direct target, but a success is already the strip.
```

The next sampler round should mutate A and B jointly: condition on one actual
bad source event, solve the two associated convex dual problems without
discarding labels, and first measure whether extremality supplies an *upper*
participation bound.  Only then ask whether the contact set obeys an all-cut
multiplicative relation.  Any candidate extraction is immediately tested
against both digit and sparse-generator Fejer models, the null-corrector, and
loss of the hereditary floor.  This is where stochastic idea generation can
be useful without relaxing rigor.

## 10. Ranked collapse ledger for the mutated KKT route

The proposed mutation is

```text
full KKT contact measure
    -> arbitrary difference graph
    -> actual-prime arithmetic terminator.
```

The exact convex statement should be frozen first.  With

```text
h(y)=sup_(t in H_Y) y dot a_P(t),
s_v=inf_(y:y dot v=-1) h(y),
```

facial reduction and compactness give the familiar primal--dual identity

```text
-s_v v = integral a_P(t) dnu(t)                  (10.1)
```

for a probability contact measure `nu` supported where an optimal separator
attains its cap (or an arbitrarily accurate version if primal attainment is
not established).  Coordinatewise,

```text
integral cos(t u_p)dnu(t)=-s_v v_p.               (10.2)
```

This part is valid convex duality.  It is also just the definition of the
radial quantity in dual coordinates, so it has not yet gained structure.

### Ranked fatal objections

| rank | proposed edge | verdict | failure certificate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | contact measure `->` spectral factor/difference graph carrying `y` | **invalid** | `h-F_y` is nonnegative only on a finite nonharmonic band; Fejer--Riesz does not apply.  The positive object is `nu`, while the coefficients `y` remain signed. |
| 2 | source/contact phases `-> B^-1` edge accuracy | **invalid** | source resolution is only `1/t_0>=Y^-1`; KKT does not force contacts near `B` or a well-conditioned diameter-`B` contact set.  Needed accuracy is `Y^-50/33`. |
| 3 | arbitrary difference graph `->` tensor SR2PF terminator | **invalid** | tensor SR2PF needs a complete Cartesian array, compatible rank-two flattenings under every cut, and weighted product labels.  An arbitrary graph supplies none of these. |
| 4 | arbitrary dense graph `->` broad SR2PF terminator | **blocked** | densification loses weights/source/floor, and broad one-flattening SR2PF remains open; additive prime grids show it contains prime-sumset hardness. |
| 5 | Bessel/KKT near equality `->` low entropy | **false abstractly** | diffuse coisometric saturation has norm `epsilon sqrt(Delta)` and stable rank `Delta`; sparse-generator Fejer states have `P_eff asyp m^2`. |
| 6 | prune graph to a useful subgraph | **unsupported** | source normalization, one-sided continuum floor, coefficient signs, ordinary-prime mask, and same-side color are not hereditary under pruning. |
| 7 | weighted bad-edge deletion `->` clean product box | **invalid** | the successful cleaning proof uses a product triangular law.  An arbitrary graph has no independent coordinate intervals, so Markov gives only a large weighted edge set, not `q^r` clean vertices. |
| 8 | graph cycle `->` integer contradiction | **insufficient at available accuracy** | an approximate length-`k` product relation has raw error about `Y^k/B`, not `<1`.  The tensor determinant works only because zero- and one-error terms cancel, giving `Y^3/B^2=Y^-1/33`. |
| 9 | prime distinctness `->` injective edge labels | **invalid** | one prime-frequency coefficient may aggregate many spectral-factor pairs.  Repeated labels let graph cycles telescope without a new prime relation. |
| 10 | Caratheodory atomicity `->` compression | **invalid** | it gives at most `#P+1` contact atoms, not `Y^(.0179+o(1))`, low stable rank, or product depth. |
| 11 | same-side reduction | **conditionally repairable** | pigeonholing a color may retain cardinality, but need not retain source leverage or the cap after renormalization. |
| 12 | KKT attainment | **technical, repairable** | compact dual attainment is available; primal recession/lineality needs facial reduction.  Approximate KKT errors must nevertheless be far below the final fixed exponent. |

### PSD Gram does not repair the first edge

Bochner positivity does give

```text
G_(p,q)=integral exp(it(u_p-u_q))dnu(t) >=0
```

as a matrix.  Equation `(10.2)` fixes only its row against the anchor
frequency zero--and, because `(10.2)` is a cosine identity, only the real
part of that row.  The Schur complement leaves an arbitrary positive
semidefinite block on the orthogonal complement, and an atomic contact
measure may have rank comparable with the number of primes.  Thus the
resulting "difference graph" is a graph of prime **ratios**, with mostly
unconstrained edge values; it is not a graph whose edges are prime tensor
entries.  Confusing these two graphs reverses the logical direction.

### Exponent closure test

To reach the existing arithmetic terminator, this route would have to prove
all of the following, with no fixed-power loss:

```text
contact/KKT data
 -> weighted frequency error <=kappa/(mB),
 -> product alphabet q asyp log log Y,
 -> depth r=(.0179-o(1))log Y/log log log Y,
 -> q^r=Y^(.0179-o(1)) clean actual-prime vertices,
 -> every-cut rank <=2.
```

Generic Caratheodory, BSG, DRC, graph removal, or a source phase net supplies
none of this ledger.  In particular a logarithmic biclique, or one matrix of
side `Y^(4/33)`, does not meet the all-cut hypothesis.

### Survivors after collapse

1. **Exact KKT contact identity:** sound, but information-neutral until a new
   inequality is applied to `(10.2)`.
2. **Direct prime-contact uncertainty theorem:** prove that every probability
   measure satisfying `(10.2)` has `s_v>=Y^(-.0179+o(1))`.  This is a clean
   formulation, but essentially the LTRAD dual target rather than a reduced
   graph theorem.
3. **Superresolved signed-factor theorem:** derive a difference graph from the
   *joint* KKT and continuum-contact data while retaining `y`, source, and
   `B^-1` accuracy.  No such nonharmonic finite-band factorization is known.
4. **Prime-labelled diffuse-isometry exclusion:** use the semiprime-gap labels
   or another actual-prime invariant to rule out the full-stable-rank KKT
   branch before seeking tensor factors.  This is the only mutation that
   addresses the newly exposed third branch.

The ranked verdict is therefore: the KKT mutation is useful as a canonical
coordinate system and numerical sampler, but the displayed three-arrow route
does not currently survive.  Its first arrow loses the primal signed data;
its last arrow asks broad SR2PF/prime-sumset mathematics unless a separate
low-entropy, all-cut theorem is inserted.  With those insertions the route
contains at least two major new lemmas, not one.

## 11. Highest-information state after the collapse

The surviving state is not "KKT gives a tensor."  It is the following honest
three-way saturation experiment:

```text
actual-prime extremal separator + exact KKT contact measure
  |
  +-- strict square-function deficit -> direct transverse return
  |
  +-- diffuse near-isometry -> new actual-prime mask-sensitive exclusion
  |
```

## 12. Fourth-round update: shared-index half-integer spacing law

One part of the diffuse Sidon branch does have an exact arithmetic
terminator.  Let cleaned vertices be

```text

```

and suppose every ordered edge is an integer on one common side with

```text
p_ij=Y exp[sigma omega(b_i-b_j)]+O(Y/B),
sigma in {+1,-1}.                                   (12.1)
```

For each consecutive triple, put `x=sigma omega g_(r+1)` and
`y=sigma omega g_r`.  The exact identity

```text
exp(x)+exp(y)-exp(x+y)-1
   =-[exp(x)-1][exp(y)-1]                           (12.2)
```

gives

```text
|(p_(r+2,r+1)+p_(r+1,r)-p_(r+2,r))-Y|
 <<Y omega^2 g_r g_(r+1)+Y/B.                       (12.3)
```

The expression in parentheses is an integer, so its distance from the
half-integer `Y` is at least `1/2`.  For large `Y`, `(12.3)` therefore forces

```text
g_r g_(r+1)>>1/(Y omega^2).                          (12.4)
```

Pair disjoint adjacent gaps and use AM--GM:

```text
diam(A)>=sum_r g_r >>M/(omega sqrt(Y)).
```

```text
W:=Y omega diam(A) >>M sqrt(Y).                      (12.5)
```

impossible when `W=o(m sqrt(Y))`.  With


this closes

```text

```

For a near-minimal Sidon ruler `rho=2tau`, the threshold is
`alpha>.5179`.  The exponential constants are uniform: the excluded regime

## 13. Fifth-round audit: the small multiplicative defect

Put

```text
Q=2Y,
d_ab=2p_ab-Q,
K_kji=d_ki-d_kj-d_ji,
E_kji=QK_kji-d_kj d_ji.
```

The proposed refinement is algebraically correct.  Direct expansion gives

```text
E_kji=Q(2p_ki)-(2p_kj)(2p_ji)
     =2(Qp_ki-2p_kj p_ji).                         (13.1)
```

At the exponential reference the right side is zero.  An entrywise prime
error `O(delta)`, with `delta=Y/B`, therefore gives

```text
|E_kji|<<Y delta=H=Y^(16/33).                      (13.2)
```

For ordinary odd primes, the expression in parentheses in `(13.1)` is odd.
Consequently

```text
E_kji=2 (mod 4),          E_kji!=0.                (13.3)
```

There is no forced sign.  In the fixed shell of logarithmic width `1/5`, no
participating prime divides the odd integer `Q`: an odd quotient `Q/p` would
lie strictly between `1` and `3`.  Reduction of `(13.1)` then proves the
stated prime/Q gcd law.  Equivalently, even without using the numerical
width, a common divisor of `E` and `Q` would have to be one of the shell
primes `p_kj,p_ji`; this is impossible because `0<|E|<<H=o(Y)asymp p`.

For fixed `j`,

```text
E_kji=-d_kj d_ji (mod Q).                          (13.4)
```

Thus every `2 x 2` minor of `E^(j)` is divisible by `Q`.  It is also
`O(H^2)`, and

```text
H^2/Q=Y^(-1/33+o(1))->0.
```

Every such minor is zero.  Since `(13.3)` excludes zero entries, every
nonempty middle slice has rational rank exactly one.  The primitive-factor
rotation argument is also valid: after writing

```text
E_kji=g_j A^(j)_k B^(j)_i,
```

one may put the unique factor `2` into `g_j`; all factors are units modulo
`Q`, and comparison with `(13.4)` supplies the claimed unit rotation.

The four-index associator is exact:

```text

```

Both sides expand to `2Q^2 p_li-8p_lk p_kj p_ji`.
