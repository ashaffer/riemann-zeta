# Shifted-Weil fixed-strip gate

Status: exact strip criterion and two analytic fail-fast theorems, 2026-08-11.
No zero-free strip, RH, or failure of a zero-free strip is proved here.
The new lemmas below are written analytic proofs and are not Lean-formalized;
no claim of priority beyond this project is made.

## 1. Verdict

There is an exact Hermitian/de Branges criterion for a prescribed closed
strip

```text
|Re rho - 1/2| <= delta,             0 <= delta < 1/2.
```

It is most cleanly stated as one block-diagonal form assembled from the
shifted-xi de Branges kernels at a sequence of shifts decreasing to `delta`.
That criterion is genuinely equivalent to the strip, including possible
zeros on its boundary.

It does **not** supply a coercive perturbation of the arithmetic RHBridge
form.  Three exact obstructions intervene before support propagation can
start.

1. The gamma factor forces the natural Fourier image of every nonzero
   compactly supported test to lie outside the shifted-xi de Branges space.
   Thus the certified `a=7/16` form and the shifted de Branges form have no
   nonzero common vectors under the identity Fourier map.
2. In the natural RHBridge-compatible class with shifted digamma/rational
   multipliers and compact pole corrections, changing the prime weight from
   `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)` to `Lambda(n)/n^(1/2+omega)` is an **indefinite**
   perturbation.  A completion-stable two-sliver construction gives both
   signs.
3. The corresponding prime-shift defect symbol still vanishes at frequency
   zero.  For `omega<1/2`, its discarded mass grows like
   `X^(1/2-omega)`, so a positive fixed shift reduces but does not cross the
   absolute-convergence barrier.

Consequently `delta>0` does not create a noncircular margin which can be
propagated from the existing certificate.  A surviving route would need a
new, nonidentity, gamma-smoothing transform together with an independently
proved exact comparison to the arithmetic form.  No such transform or
comparison is presently available.

## 2. One exact Hermitian form for the closed strip

Write `xi` for the completed Riemann xi function and put

```text
E_omega(z)  = xi(1/2 + omega - i z),
E_omega#(z) = conjugate(E_omega(conjugate z))
            = xi(1/2 - omega - i z).
```

The entire de Branges kernel is

```text
K_omega(z,w)
  = [E_omega(z) conjugate(E_omega(w))
       - E_omega#(z) conjugate(E_omega#(w))]
      / [2 pi i (conjugate(w)-z)].                         (2.1)
```

The diagonal is interpreted by continuation when needed.  For
`z=x+iy`, `y>0`, no limiting convention is needed and

```text
K_omega(z,z)
  = [|E_omega(z)|^2-|E_omega#(z)|^2]/(4 pi y).             (2.2)
```

Fix `0<=delta<1/2` and choose

```text
omega_m = delta + (1/2-delta)/(m+1),       m=1,2,... .     (2.3)
```

Let `V_delta` be the algebraic direct sum, over `m`, of the free complex
vector spaces generated by points of the upper half-plane.  Thus an element
has only finitely many components and every component is a finite formal sum

```text
v_m = sum_j c_(m,j) [z_(m,j)].
```

Define the single Hermitian form

```text
H_delta(v,v)
  = sum_m sum_(j,k) c_(m,j) conjugate(c_(m,k))
      K_(omega_m)(z_(m,j),z_(m,k)).                        (2.4)
```

### Theorem 2.1 (countable-block fixed-strip criterion)

The following are equivalent.

1. Every nontrivial zeta zero satisfies
   `|Re rho-1/2|<=delta`.
2. `H_delta(v,v)>=0` for every `v in V_delta`.

#### Proof

Suppose first that the strip holds.  Suzuki's shifted-xi criterion says that

```text
Theta_omega = E_omega#/E_omega
```

is meromorphic inner in the upper half-plane for every `omega>delta`.
Equivalently, `E_omega` is Hermite--Biehler and (2.1) is positive
semidefinite.  This applies to every `omega_m`, and hence to their algebraic
direct sum (2.4).

Conversely, suppose that a zero

```text
rho = 1/2+d+i gamma,        d>delta,
```

exists; functional-equation symmetry lets us choose the zero on the right.
For every sufficiently large `m`, `omega_m<d`, and

```text
z_m = -gamma + i(d-omega_m)
```

lies in the upper half-plane with `E_(omega_m)(z_m)=xi(rho)=0`.  Moreover,

```text
E_(omega_m)#(z_m) = xi(rho-2 omega_m).                     (2.5)
```

The points `rho-2 omega_m` are distinct and converge to `rho-2 delta`.
They cannot all be zeros of the nonzero entire function `xi`.  Choose a
large `m` for which the right side of (2.5) is nonzero.  Equation (2.2) then
gives

```text
K_(omega_m)(z_m,z_m)
  = -|E_(omega_m)#(z_m)|^2/[4 pi(d-omega_m)] < 0.
```

The one-atom vector in that block contradicts (2).  This proves the right
half of the strip, and functional-equation symmetry gives the left half.

The use of a sequence `omega_m downarrow delta` is deliberate.  It handles
zeros on the boundary and also removes any possible cancellation caused by a
horizontal pair of zeros separated by exactly `2 omega` at one exceptional
shift.  Positivity at one arbitrarily selected endpoint kernel is not the
clean closed-strip statement; (2.4) is.

## 3. Exact lack of a coercive seed

### 3.1 The kernel itself has no raw atom floor

For fixed `omega>0` and fixed `y>0`, Stirling's formula and the standard
polynomial vertical-strip bound for zeta give

```text
xi(sigma+i t)
  = O_(sigma)((1+|t|)^A exp(-pi|t|/4))                    (3.1)
```

on each fixed vertical line.  Apply this to the two xi factors in (2.2).
For some `A=A(omega,y)`,

```text
K_omega(x+iy,x+iy)
  = O((1+|x|)^A exp(-pi|x|/2)) -> 0.                      (3.2)
```

Thus, even in the unconditional Hermite--Biehler range `omega>1/2`, there is
no `c>0` such that the kernel quadratic form dominates `c` times the raw
coefficient `ell^2` norm of all point atoms.  Kernel positivity supplies its
own reproducing-kernel norm; it does not supply an external coercive norm for
the support-transfer theorem.

There is also an endpoint-specific collapse which a closed-strip criterion
must allow.  If `rho=1/2+delta+i gamma` is a boundary zero of multiplicity
`m` and `epsilon=omega-delta downarrow 0`, local factorization of `xi` in
(4.1) gives

```text
K_omega(-gamma,-gamma)
  = c_rho epsilon^(2m-1) + O(epsilon^(2m)),       c_rho>0.
```

Thus the component margins in the exact block criterion necessarily collapse
when an allowed zero lies on the strip boundary.  Positivity of the closed
criterion cannot itself furnish a uniform reserve away from zero.

### 3.2 Gamma-decay domain-separation theorem

The more decisive mismatch is at the level of the actual RHBridge tests.

### Theorem 3.1

Fix `a<infinity` and `omega>0`.  Let `f in L^2(-a,a)`, extend it by zero, and
write

```text
F(t) = integral f(x) exp(i t x) dx.
```

If the boundary quotient `F/E_omega` belongs to `L^2(R)`, then `f=0`.
In particular, whenever the de Branges space is defined,

```text
{Fourier transforms of compactly supported L^2 tests} intersect B(E_omega)
  = {0}.                                                    (3.3)
```

#### Proof

By (3.1), for fixed `omega` there are `C,A` such that

```text
|E_omega(t)|
  <= C(1+|t|)^A exp(-pi|t|/4),             t real.         (3.4)
```

Hence `F/E_omega in L^2` implies

```text
integral |F(t)|^2 exp(pi|t|/2)/(1+|t|)^(2A) dt < infinity. (3.5)
```

For every `c<pi/4`, the polynomial in (3.5) can be absorbed into the spare
exponential, so `exp(c|t|)F(t)` is in `L^2`.  Fourier inversion and
Cauchy--Schwarz now extend `f` holomorphically to `|Im z|<c`: the inverse
integral is absolutely convergent on every smaller closed strip.  Its real
boundary value agrees with `f` almost everywhere, and hence vanishes almost
everywhere on the two open rays outside `[-a,a]`.  Continuity upgrades this
to pointwise vanishing there; the identity theorem forces the extension, and
therefore `f`, to vanish identically.

Membership in `B(E_omega)` requires `F/E_omega in H^2`, hence the boundary
`L^2` condition used above.  This proves (3.3).

### Consequence for the certified base

The `a=7/16` theorem is a coercive lower bound for the arithmetic Weil form on
the logarithmic Fourier domain of compactly supported tests.  Theorem 3.1
shows that its natural Fourier image provides **no nonzero seed vector** in
any of the de Branges spaces occurring in (2.4).  Therefore neither
`UniformSupportTransfer` nor `SupportDecomposition` can be instantiated by
identifying their old block with a subspace of `B(E_omega)`.

A future adapter would have to be nonidentity and exponentially smoothing at
the gamma scale.  It would also have to prove, independently of the desired
strip, an exact relation between its de Branges norm and the arithmetic
pole--archimedean--prime form.  Merely writing down `B(E_omega)` does not
provide that relation.

## 4. Prime, archimedean, and pole audit

Away from zeros on the real boundary, (2.1) has the exact diagonal formula;
at a zero the product is interpreted by its removable continuation:

```text
K_omega(t,t)
  = |xi(sigma-i t)|^2/pi * Re[(xi'/xi)(sigma-i t)],
sigma = 1/2+omega.                                        (4.1)
```

For `sigma>1`, ordinary absolute convergence permits the expansion

```text
Re (xi'/xi)(s)
 = Re[1/s+1/(s-1)]
   - (1/2)log pi
   + (1/2) Re psi(s/2)
   - sum_(n>=2) Lambda(n)n^(-sigma) cos(t log n).          (4.2)
```

This records all four pieces exactly:

- the completed-function pole factors are the rational multiplier
  `Re[1/s+1/(s-1)]`;
- the archimedean term is the shifted digamma at real part
  `1/4+omega/2`;
- the prime weights are `Lambda(n)n^(-1/2-omega)`;
- the entire expression is multiplied by `|xi(s)|^2` in (4.1).

It is not the RHBridge form.  In particular, the RHBridge pole term is a
rank-two moment form, its prime weight is `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`, and its
archimedean multiplier acts directly on the arbitrary test Fourier energy.
The kernel (2.1) is a two-variable, non-translation-invariant xi-product
kernel.

Every exact criterion (2.4) for `delta<1/2` contains shifts
`omega_m<1/2`, hence `sigma_m<1`.  In that range the prime series in (4.2)
is not an ordinary Dirichlet series and cannot be used term by term.  Formula
(4.1) remains entire because `|xi|^2` cancels logarithmic-derivative poles,
but separating that cancellation below the absolute-convergence line either
requires a contour continuation with zero residues or assumes the relevant
zero-free region.  This is precisely where a nominal prime decomposition
becomes circular.

There are therefore two honest choices, neither of which propagates the
existing certificate:

1. keep the exact xi-product kernel (2.1), in which case Theorem 3.1 gives a
   form-domain mismatch and there is no RHBridge old block; or
2. replace it by a translation-invariant arithmetic shadow with damped prime
   weights, in which case the next theorem shows that the shift is not a
   positive perturbation.

## 5. Completion-stable two-sliver no-monotonicity theorem

Let `C_h(f)=Re integral f(x) conjugate(f(x+h)) dx` for zero-extended complex
tests on a fixed bounded interval.  Consider a Hermitian form difference

```text
D(f)
  = integral r(xi)|fHat(xi)|^2 dxi
    + <f,F f>
    + sum_(h in S) 2 q_h C_h(f),                            (5.1)
```

where

- `S` is a finite set of distinct positive shifts;
- `r` is real-valued and even, is bounded, and tends to zero at both
  frequency infinities; and
- `F` is compact and self-adjoint (finite-rank pole corrections are enough
  here); for the final restriction to real RHBridge tests, it also commutes
  with complex conjugation.

### Theorem 5.1

Suppose `q_u != 0` for one `u in S` and the interval contains two small
translated subintervals `I` and `I+u`.  They may be chosen so small that no
other shift in `S` connects either subinterval to the other.  Then `D` takes
both signs on unit vectors.  More precisely, there are unit vectors
`f_(N,+)` and `f_(N,-)` such that

```text
lim_(N->infinity) D(f_(N,+)) =  q_u,
lim_(N->infinity) D(f_(N,-)) = -q_u.                       (5.2)
```

#### Proof

Choose a smooth unit bump `g` supported in `I`, let
`g_N(x)=exp(iNx)g(x)`, and let
`Jg_N` be its exact translate to `I+u`.  Put

```text
f_(N,+/-) = (g_N +/- Jg_N)/sqrt(2).
```

The supports are disjoint, so these are unit vectors, and the exact
two-sliver identities give

```text
C_u(f_(N,+))  =  1/2,
C_u(f_(N,-))  = -1/2.
```

All the other correlations in (5.1) vanish by the choice of `I`.
Modulation translates the fixed Fourier profile to frequency `N`; bounded
convergence and `r(xi)->0` make the multiplier term tend to zero.  The two
sequences converge weakly to zero, so compactness of `F` makes the second
term tend to zero.  The remaining term is exactly `+/- q_u`, proving (5.2).
Although the construction is written in the natural complex Hermitian
space, the same sign conclusion holds after restriction to real tests: the
complexified value is the sum of the values on the real and imaginary
parts, so a value of either strict sign supplies a real component with that
sign.

### Corollary 5.2 (fixed strip damping is not a positive update)

More precisely, consider any RHBridge-compatible shifted arithmetic shadow

```text
Q~_(a,omega)(f)
  = P_(a,omega)(f)
    + integral m_omega(xi)|fHat(xi)|^2 dxi
    - sum_(log n<2a) 2 Lambda(n)n^(-1/2-omega)
        C_(log n)(f),                                       (5.3)
```

for which `P_(a,omega)-P_(a,0)` is represented by a compact operator commuting
with conjugation and
`m_omega-m_0` is real, even, bounded, and tends to zero at both frequency
infinities.
This includes the customary finite-rank shifted pole moments, the rational
completed-pole correction in (4.2), and the shifted-minus-unshifted digamma
multiplier.

The obvious arithmetic shadow of a positive shift changes a prime coefficient
from

```text
w_0(n)     = Lambda(n)n^(-1/2)
```

to

```text
w_omega(n) = Lambda(n)n^(-1/2-omega).
```

Since the Weil form contains `-2w(n)C_(log n)`, its prime-part difference is

```text
2 [w_0(n)-w_omega(n)] C_(log n),                            (5.4)
```

with a nonzero positive coefficient.  The shifted-minus-unshifted digamma
multiplier is bounded and tends to zero at high frequency, by the digamma
asymptotic.  The usual change of pole moments is finite rank; the rational
completed-pole multiplier in (4.2) also tends to zero and may instead be
absorbed into `r`.  Thus Theorem 5.1 applies to
`Q~_(a,omega)-Q~_(a,0)`, not only to an isolated prime summand.

At the certified base, `log 2 < 2(7/16)=7/8`, and `2` is the only active
prime power.  Two translated slivers therefore fit inside `[-7/16,7/16]`
with no competing prime shift.  Here the surviving coefficient is exactly

```text
q_2 = (log 2)/sqrt(2) * (1-2^(-omega)) > 0.
```

Consequently, already on the certified window, every shifted shadow in the
displayed class gives neither

```text
Q~_(a,omega) >= Q~_(a,0)
```

nor the reverse Loewner inequality, even after the stated shifted
archimedean and pole corrections.  In particular, `delta>0` is not an added
coercive mass which can simply be combined with the certified margin.

The same construction can be placed directly in an adjacent old/collar
split.  At an activation threshold `u=2a`, take `I` just inside the left edge
of `[-a,a]` and `I+u` in the new right collar of `[-b,b]`, `b>a`.  Then the
two packets are respectively old and collar vectors, while their selected
prime cross coefficient remains `q_u`, independent of the collar width.
Taking smooth bumps away from all interval endpoints keeps both packets in
the logarithmic form domain.  Thus the obstruction is not an artifact of a
sharp projection leaving that domain.

This theorem does not say that a fully completed shifted form is negative.
It says that any proof of its positivity must retain a genuinely joint
prime--archimedean--pole cancellation; monotone perturbation and separate
piecewise bounds are ruled out.

## 6. Zero-frequency and absolute-convergence barrier

For a finite prime cutoff `X`, the nonnegative defect symbol associated with
the damped weights is

```text
A_(omega,X)(xi)
  = sum_(n<=X) 2 Lambda(n)n^(-1/2-omega)
      [1-cos(xi log n)].                                    (6.1)
```

Exactly as in `ActivationCancellation`,

```text
A_(omega,X)(0)=0.                                           (6.2)
```

Hence no positive pointwise `L^2` floor can come from the shifted prime
defect alone.  Rewriting correlations as defect energy also leaves the mass

```text
M_omega(X)=sum_(n<=X) Lambda(n)n^(-1/2-omega).
```

The prime number theorem and partial summation give, for
`0<=omega<1/2`,

```text
M_omega(X) ~ X^(1/2-omega)/(1/2-omega).                    (6.3)
```

At a support cutoff `X=exp(2a)`, this is exponential in `a` with exponent
`1-2omega`.  At `omega=1/2` it is logarithmic, and only for
`omega>1/2` is the corresponding infinite log-derivative prime series
absolutely convergent.  Thus a fixed `omega<1/2` weakens the zero-frequency
mass but does not remove it.  The threshold at which this elementary route
becomes safe is exactly the already-unconditional half-plane `Re s>1`.

The two requested stress tests therefore agree:

- two slivers rule out operator-norm smallness and Loewner monotonicity at
  each activation;
- zero frequency rules out a positive defect-symbol floor after summing the
  activations.

## 7. Consequence for the support-propagation program

The exact form (2.4) is useful as a clean target and as a negative-square
detector, but it does not instantiate the current support-transfer package.
The obstruction occurs before the Schur determinant `c^2<beta*d` can even be
posed with the certified old block:

```text
arithmetic base Q_(7/16)
    --identity Fourier map--> shifted de Branges block

has only the zero vector in its natural common domain.                    (7.1)
```

Nor can one repair (7.1) by declaring the shifted prime weights to be a
positive correction: Theorem 5.1 gives an infinite-dimensional, completion-
stable sign obstruction, while (6.2) excludes a defect-symbol mass floor.

A shifted-Weil propagation theorem would therefore require all of the
following genuinely new data.

1. An explicit nonidentity transform `U_(a,omega)` which supplies the gamma
   smoothing forced by Theorem 3.1 and preserves the logarithmic form domain.
2. An independently derived identity or one-sided comparison between
   `Q_a` and the pullback of (2.1), with prime, archimedean, and pole pieces
   kept together.
3. Old/collar compatibility of `U_(a,omega)` under zero extension.
4. A strict Schur estimate which survives the two-sliver sequences and the
   zero-frequency mode without assuming positivity of the target block.

Absent such an adapter, this branch should not replace the current fixed-box
Type-II target.  The useful new conclusion is narrower and exact: the
shifted-xi kernel gives the right fixed-strip criterion, but its gamma-scale
domain and its indefinite arithmetic shadow prevent the existing local Weil
certificate from serving as a propagation seed.
