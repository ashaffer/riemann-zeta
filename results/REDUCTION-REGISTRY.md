# RH reduction registry

This registry separates proved reductions from research candidates.  A row is
not promoted until its assumptions are stated independently of RH and every
arrow has either a proof or a named literature source.

## Status vocabulary

- **proved-local**: proved in this repository, but may consume a named
  classical literature axiom;
- **known**: established in the literature, not newly proved here;
- **candidate**: a precise implication remains to be proved;
- **diagnostic**: useful numerically but not a logical implication;
- **circular**: the premise assumes RH or an equivalent positivity statement.

## Promotion checklist

For a candidate `C -> RH`, record:

1. a formal statement of `C` that does not mention RH-dependent objects
   without qualification;
2. the exact intermediate inequality or nondegeneracy theorem;
3. whether `C` is known to imply RH by another route;
4. countermodels or synthetic tests that separate `C` from the conclusion;
5. primary literature establishing prior connections;
6. which steps are formal, assumed from consensus literature, numerical, or
   conjectural.
