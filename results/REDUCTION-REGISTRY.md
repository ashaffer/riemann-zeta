# RH reduction registry

This chronological registry separates proved reductions from research
candidates.  A row is not promoted until its assumptions are stated
independently of RH and every arrow has either a proof or a named literature
source.  The canonical deduplication by RH carrier, including the exact arrow
direction of every row, is maintained in
[`RH-PROXY-LEDGER.md`](RH-PROXY-LEDGER.md).
The analytic theorem floor for the R65--R98 branch is maintained separately in
[`publication/IMPORTED-ANALYTIC-BASELINE.md`](../publication/IMPORTED-ANALYTIC-BASELINE.md);
recovering one of those imported bounds is not a new registry milestone.

## Status vocabulary

- **proved-local**: proved in this repository, but may consume a named
  classical literature axiom;
- **known**: established in the literature, not newly proved here;
- **candidate**: a precise implication remains to be proved;
- **diagnostic**: useful numerically but not a logical implication;
- **circular**: the premise assumes RH or an equivalent positivity statement.
