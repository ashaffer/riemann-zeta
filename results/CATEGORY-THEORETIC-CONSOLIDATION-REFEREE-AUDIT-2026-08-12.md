# Category-theoretic consolidation: hostile referee audit

Status: structural synthesis and scope audit, 2026-08-12.  The statements
below are exact finite-dimensional lemmas unless an infinite-dimensional
hypothesis is explicitly stated.  They organize existing results; they prove
neither a uniform zero-free strip nor its failure.

## 1. Referee verdict

Most of the repository's apparently different failures can honestly be
compressed into four order-enriched categorical lemmas:

1. **Probe-cone descent.**  Positivity descends from a family of local tests
   exactly when the positive rank-one cones generated by those tests fill the
   whole positive cone.  Linear spanning, topological totality, and ordinary
   cover descent are not enough.
2. **Conservative completion transport.**  Invertible pullbacks, positive
   Schur reductions, Birman--Schwinger transforms, and sign-preserving
   functional calculus conserve the negative index.  Transformations which
   make the sign easier can erase the negative line.  A nonlinear spectral
   operation cannot also distribute over the place sum, except in the affine
   case.
3. **Aligned-face separation.**  On the positive-null face, a completed row
   dominated by the negative carrier cannot be canceled by any positive
   ensemble unless a carrier-sized positive component comes from the genuine
   transverse remainder.  This is the common theorem behind the
   multi-witness, phase, quadrature, direct-sum, and soft-nulling no-go results.
4. **Decategorification--polarization separation.**  A signed trace or
   supertrace factors through a nonconservative decategorification and is
   blind to arbitrary contractible cross-place glue.  Replacing it by one
   faithful positive trace and imposing the required pure and mixed Euler
   moments forces orthogonal block locality.  A positive polarization must be
   separately constructed; it cannot be recovered from the trace identity.

These combine into a useful but deliberately scoped meta-theorem:

> A proof assembled only from local probes, positive mixtures,
> completion-compatible changes of representation, and signed trace
> identities cannot remove a completed negative carrier.  It must add at
> least one of: an order-reflecting coherent probe family, a carrier-scale
> transverse arithmetic estimate, an independently constructed positive
> polarization, or a cofinal quantitative limit theorem.

This is not an impossibility theorem for RH or for a zero-free strip.  It is a
classification of the operations actually audited in this repository.  Its
value is that it reduces a large search tree to the location of the one
missing arrow: a zeta-specific source of order transverse to the completed
carrier.

The four lemmas are genuinely distinct.  In particular, Lemma 4 should not be
folded into Lemma 2.  Lemma 2 concerns transporting an already given ordered
Hermitian form.  Lemma 4 concerns the invalid reconstruction of an ordered
polarization from an equality-valued decategorification.

## 2. The smallest honest categorical container

Let `FHilb_fd` be the dagger category of finite-dimensional complex Hilbert
spaces.  Over each object `V`, retain the real ordered vector space

```text
Herm(V),             with cone Pos(V)={K:K>=0}.       (2.1)
```

A linear map `S:W->V` gives contravariant pullback

```text
S^*: Herm(V)->Herm(W),       K |-> S^* K S.           (2.2)
```

Positive states are matrices `Gamma>=0`, paired with effects by

```text
<K,Gamma>=Tr(K Gamma).                                (2.3)
```

Direct sum is the symmetric monoidal operation.  The operator norm and the
support/depth filtration are extra enrichments; they are not consequences of
the bare dagger category.

At a finite Gabor stage the explicit formula is an equality of effects,

```text
K_comp=K_pole+K_arch-K_prime=K_selected+K_collateral. (2.4)
```

Admissible changes of test space pull both sides back by the same map.  This
is the categorical content of completion.  It is equality-producing
structure, not order-producing structure.

Three warnings are essential.

1. The relevant object is an **ordered, norm-enriched Hermitian fibration**
   over test spaces, not a bare category of vector spaces.
2. The Hermitian Witt group is too coarse.  Over `C` with complex
   conjugation, a form with one positive and one negative line is
   hyperbolic/Witt-trivial, although it is indefinite.  The
   normalized mirror block has exactly this shape.  One must retain full
   inertia `(n_+,n_-,n_0)`, or equivalently rank together with signature and
   nullity, rather than only its Witt class.
3. The vector-space assignment to support intervals may have extension maps,
   but the quadratic form is nonlocal.  It is not a sheaf of positive cones on
   ordinary interval covers: cross terms between separated pieces are data,
   not gluing noise.

## 3. Lemma 1: probe-cone descent

### 3.1 Exact statement

Let `V` be finite-dimensional, and let `j_i:V_i->V` be any family of linear
probes.  Define the pushed-forward local positive cone

```text
C_J=closure cone{j_i Gamma_i j_i^*: Gamma_i>=0}.     (3.1)
```

Then the following are equivalent.

1. For every `K in Herm(V)`, the local conditions

   ```text
   j_i^* K j_i>=0              for all i             (3.2)
   ```

   imply `K>=0`.
2. `C_J=Pos(V)`.

For a family of vector tests `S subset V`, take `V_i=C` and `j_i(1)=s_i`.
Then (3.1) becomes

```text
C_S=closure cone{s s^*:s in S}.                     (3.3)
```

Thus nonnegativity of `s^*Ks` on `S` detects positivity of every Hermitian
`K` exactly when `C_S=Pos(V)`.  Since the extreme rays of `Pos(V)` are all
rank-one rays `v v^*`, a compact normalized probe family must be projectively
dense in all directions.  Merely having `span(S)=V` is insufficient.

### 3.2 Proof

The dual cone of `C_J` under the trace pairing is precisely

```text
C_J^*={K:j_i^*Kj_i>=0 for every i}.                 (3.4)
```

The PSD cone is closed and self-dual.  Hence (3.2) implies `K>=0` for all `K`
if and only if `C_J^*=Pos(V)`, equivalently `C_J=Pos(V)` by the bipolar
theorem.  This proves the statement.

### 3.3 Exact counterexample to overbroad descent

The real symmetric Toeplitz matrix

```text
    [1   0  5/4]
T = [0   1   0 ]                                    (3.5)
    [5/4 0   1 ]
```

is positive on every consecutive modulated box in `C^3`, including the
full-width vectors `(1,e^(i theta),e^(2i theta))`, but

```text
(1,0,-1) T (1,0,-1)^T=-1/2.                         (3.6)
```

Every proper consecutive principal block is positive.  Thus even a cover by
locally positive subspaces and a total family of individual vectors does not
give order descent.  The missing datum is the polarized cross term between
the first and third coordinates.

### 3.4 Results subsumed

This lemma contains the categorical core of:

- the triangular/Selberg-packet cone no-go;
- failure of translated short-window positivity to glue;
- failure of a fixed or proper test bank to find a movable negative line;
- failure of global trace/Frobenius moments to determine a selected
  compression;
- the need for coherent sums, rather than positivity on their individual
  summands; and
- the fact that positive convex mixtures add no new tested ray beyond their
  constituents.

The quantitative low-minor bound

```text
r <= N/(1+3*kappa)                                  (3.7)
```

is stronger than Lemma 1 and is not categorical.  It uses the size and
delocalization of a particular hidden negative vector.  Lemma 1 explains why
a localization theorem is necessary; it does not supply the order in (3.7).

## 4. Lemma 2: conservative completion transport

### 4.1 Exact statement

For a finite Hermitian operator `K`, the following hold.

1. **Invertible pullback.**  If `S` is invertible, then

   ```text
   inertia(S^*KS)=inertia(K).                        (4.1)
   ```

   If `S` is not onto, positivity of `S^*KS` need not imply positivity of
   `K`; the range can avoid the negative line.
2. **Positive stabilization and reduction.**  If

   ```text
   H=[[A,B],[B^*,D]],                  D>0,           (4.2)
   ```

   then

   ```text
   n_-(H)=n_-(A-BD^(-1)B^*).                         (4.3)
   ```

   Adding a positive auxiliary sector and eliminating it therefore preserves
   the unresolved negative index.
3. **Sign-preserving spectral calculus.**  If `f(0)=0` and
   `sign f(x)=sign x` for `x!=0`, then

   ```text
   n_-(f(K))=n_-(K).                                 (4.4)
   ```

   By contrast, `K^2`, `abs(K)`, and `exp(-tK^2)` erase the sign and are not
   order-reflecting.
4. **Additivity of placewise spectral calculus.**  If a continuous scalar
   function satisfies

   ```text
   f(A+B)=f(A)+f(B)-f(0)                             (4.5)
   ```

   for every commuting finite Hermitian `A,B`, then

   ```text
   f(x)=f(0)+c*x.                                    (4.6)
   ```

   Thus a genuinely nonlinear spectral amplifier either acts on the full
   completed operator, where it preserves or erases the same sign, or is
   applied separately to the place pieces, where it creates unaccounted mixed
   prime--prime, prime--gamma, and pole--prime terms.

### 4.2 Proof

Part 1 is Sylvester inertia.  Part 2 is block Gaussian congruence
(Haynsworth additivity).  Part 3 is the spectral theorem.  For Part 4, apply
(4.5) to scalar matrices and put `g=f-f(0)`; continuity and Cauchy additivity
give `g(x)=cx`.

### 4.3 Minimal countermodels to overbroad naturality

For the normalized mirror block

```text
M=m[[1,C],[C,1]],                C>1,                (4.7)
```

dephasing is a natural conditional expectation for the chosen block
decomposition, but

```text
E(M)=m I>0,                 while M has one negative line. (4.8)
```

Projection to the positive eigenline has the same failure.  These maps are
positive but not conservative/order-reflecting.  Conversely an invertible
preconditioner retains the negative line exactly.  A Schur complement may
display a larger unnormalized scalar pivot, but the harmonic lift carries the
correspondingly larger norm; the normalized Rayleigh quotient has not been
amplified for free.

### 4.4 Results subsumed

This lemma contains the categorical core of:

- invertible preconditioning and nonunitary metric changes;
- positive-block Schur, Feshbach, Hodge, and Dirichlet-to-Neumann lifts;
- Birman--Schwinger and resolvent reformulations;
- squaring, absolute value, heat smoothing, and sign functional calculus;
- random block dephasing when regarded as a nonconservative conditional
  expectation; and
- the failure of a nonlinear place-by-place transform to preserve the
  completed explicit formula.

It does not subsume the analytic domain-separation theorem for the shifted-xi
de Branges space.  That result says there is no nonzero admissible
intertwining object between two specific analytic categories.  Category
theory can state the missing functor; gamma decay and Paley--Wiener theory
prove its absence.

## 5. Lemma 3: aligned-face separation

### 5.1 Exact statement

Let `P,N>=0` and let `B=B_align+R` be Hermitian effects on `V`.  Define the
positive-null face

```text
F_P={Gamma>=0:Tr(P Gamma)=0}.                        (5.1)
```

Let `V_0=ker P`, and suppose on `V_0` that

```text
B_align<=-c N,                         c>0.          (5.2)
```

Then every `Gamma in F_P` satisfying aggregate cancellation

```text
Tr(B Gamma)=0                                         (5.3)
```

obeys

```text
c Tr(N Gamma)
 <= Tr(R Gamma)
 <= ||(R_0)_+||_op Tr(Gamma),                        (5.4)
```

where `R_0` is the compression of `R` to `V_0`.

In particular:

- if `R=0`, aggregate cancellation kills the entire carrier;
- if `||(R_0)_+||=o(K)` for normalized states, no carrier of size `K`
  survives; and
- a carrier-surviving state requires a positive transverse remainder of
  carrier scale.

Conversely, if there are positive states `Gamma_-`,`Gamma_+` in `F_P` with

```text
b_-=Tr(B Gamma_-)<0<b_+=Tr(B Gamma_+),               (5.5)
```

then the positive mixture

```text
Gamma=[b_+ Gamma_-+|b_-| Gamma_+]/[b_++|b_-|]       (5.6)
```

is aggregate-null.  It yields a negative full form `Q` exactly when

```text
b_+[-Tr(Q Gamma_-)]>|b_-|Tr(Q Gamma_+).              (5.7)
```

Thus the carrier-scale transverse state, together with its full-form cost,
is not only necessary but sufficient for this positive-ensemble mechanism.

### 5.2 Proof

For `Gamma>=0`, `Tr(P Gamma)=0` implies that `Gamma` is supported in `ker P`:
the positive matrix `P^(1/2) Gamma P^(1/2)` has trace zero and hence vanishes.
On that face, (5.2)--(5.3) give

```text
c Tr(N Gamma)<=Tr(R Gamma).                          (5.8)
```

The positive-part bound gives the second inequality in (5.4).  Equations
(5.6)--(5.7) are direct substitution.

### 5.3 Categorical interpretation

Positive multi-witnesses are states in the completely-positive completion of
`FHilb`; their covariance is one `Gamma>=0`.  Classical randomization is a
convex combination of such states.  A coherent superposition is already one
pure state and carries all off-diagonal terms.  Equation (5.2) is an order
relation between effects on a face of the state cone.  Direct sums and
positive mixtures preserve it.

This is the precise point where the category-theoretic collapse stops.  The
existence or absence of `(R_0)_+` at scale `K` is an operator-norm estimate,
not a formal property of states and effects.

### 5.4 Results subsumed

The lemma contains the common proof behind:

- positive multi-witness and multi-seed ensembles;
- phase and real/imaginary quadrature variants;
- positive direct sums over packets or support scales;
- randomization that retains only positive state weights;
- the aligned mirror-row obstruction after the positive row is nulled; and
- the exact dichotomy between an aligned face and a two-state transverse
  reservoir.

The soft-leakage variant has the still simpler identity

```text
M=mI+B.                                              (5.9)
```

For `Gamma>=0`, canceling `Tr(B Gamma)` makes
`Tr(M Gamma)=m Tr(Gamma)>=0`; positive-row leakage does not rescue the
selected mirror contribution.

In the actual zeta problem, `R` consists of the collateral-zero,
actual-prime, pole/archimedean, overlap, and coherent inter-scale terms left
after the aligned selected block.  The repository proves that sufficiently
shallow collateral rows are `o(K)`, but one almost-equally-deep pair can be a
carrier-scale abstract reservoir.  That depth statement is analytic; Lemma 3
only identifies exactly where it enters.

## 6. Lemma 4: decategorification--polarization separation

### 6.1 Signed-trace invisibility

Let `C` be a finite `Z/2`-graded Hilbert complex with equivariant chain
endomorphisms.  Supertrace factors through equivariant `K_0`, hence through
cohomology.  In particular, for any finite Hilbert space `E`, the contractible
bridge

```text
E_even --Id--> E_odd                                 (6.1)
```

has zero supertrace for every chain endomorphism which acts by the same matrix
on its even and odd copies.  Therefore arbitrary positive-metric,
noncommuting cross-place bridge matrices may be added in (6.1) without
changing the supertrace of any word in those matrices.

Consequently no collection of signed trace identities alone determines:

- whether cross-place extensions are present;
- whether they survive in cohomology;
- a positive polarization; or
- an order relation on the resulting zero detector.

This is a nonconservativity theorem, not merely an observation that one trace
has too few moments.

### 6.2 Faithful-positive-trace alternative

Let `tau` be a faithful finite positive trace and let `0<=P,Q<=I`.  If

```text
tau(P)=tau(P^2),       tau(Q)=tau(Q^2),
tau(PQ)=0,                                           (6.2)
```

then `P,Q` are orthogonal projections.

Indeed, `P-P^2>=0` and faithfulness make `P=P^2`; similarly for `Q`.
Then `tau(PQP)=tau(PQ)=0`, so positivity and faithfulness give `PQP=0`, hence
`QP=PQ=0`.

Thus one faithful positive trace which both saturates the pure Euler moments
and deletes the first mixed moment forces the local sectors to split
orthogonally.  It supplies no cross-place positivity mechanism.  A signed
trace can cancel mixed sectors virtually, but the cancellation carries no
positive meaning.

### 6.3 Polarization circularity

For a finite complex matrix `A`, the following are equivalent:

1. there is `G>0` with

   ```text
   A^*G+GA=G;                                        (6.3)
   ```

2. `A-(1/2)I` is similar to a skew-Hermitian matrix;
3. `A` is diagonalizable and every eigenvalue has real part `1/2`.

The proof conjugates by `G^(1/2)`.  Therefore fitting a positive polarization
after constructing the generator is exactly the desired spectral conclusion.
In a successful categorical model the polarization must be independent
geometric input, as the Rosati form is in the function-field control.

Functional-equation duality is strictly weaker.  For a nonzero real `a`, set

```text
A_a=diag(1/2+a,1/2-a),
Omega=[[0,1],[-1,0]],                                (6.4)
```

one has `A_a^T Omega+Omega A_a=Omega`, while no positive
`G` satisfies (6.3).  Duality or Poincare-type pairing alone is compatible
with an off-line reflected pair.

### 6.4 Results subsumed

This lemma contains the categorical core of:

- the two-prime/global-trace polarization gates;
- direct-sum cyclotomic trace reconstructions;
- supertrace, plethystic-logarithm, and cumulant cancellation of mixed states;
- the failure of higher contractible glue to create a cohomological metric;
- the distinction between an explicit/Lefschetz trace and a Hodge/Rosati
  polarization; and
- the warning that a spectral metric computed from the desired eigenvectors
  has assumed the conclusion.

The related degree-zero Hodge identity is another exact shadow of the same
separation.  For a complex beginning in degree zero,

```text
Delta_0=d_0^*d_0,                                   (6.5)
```

so later differentials cannot improve the degree-zero form without changing
the primary map or the polarization.

## 7. Filtered-colimit addendum: useful bookkeeping, not an engine

Let `(V_i,q_i)` be a filtered system with exact form-preserving embeddings,
and suppose the algebraic colimit is the union of the stages.  Then

```text
q>=0 on colim V_i   iff   q_i>=0 for every i.        (7.1)
```

A cofinal subsystem suffices.  To extend (7.1) from an algebraic form core to
a closed infinite-dimensional form, one additionally needs closability and
form-core density.

This explains the correct role of finite Lean certificates.  An arbitrary
finite set of positive stages is not cofinal.  A finite proof of all stages
would require a certified recursive transfer step.  Category theory identifies
that missing natural transformation but does not prove its analytic estimate.
Nor does (7.1) give a uniform positive margin: stage margins may decrease to
zero while every stage and the colimit remain nonnegative.

This addendum should not be advertised as an RH lemma.  It prevents invalid
finite-to-global inference and specifies the shape of a valid certificate
tower.

## 8. What the categorical invariants do not remember

The following are indispensable analytic quantities, not consequences of the
four lemmas.

| Quantity/result | Missing noncategorical input |
|---|---|
| carrier `K=X^(alpha d-o(1))/L` | Paley--Wiener growth and packet normalization |
| shallow-depth gate `beta<=alpha d-epsilon` | strip sampling and local zero counts |
| order `N/(1+3 kappa)` before minors see a flat defect | quantitative eigenvector mass and dimension |
| endpoint-jet/collar error `o(K)` | approximation rates and support geometry |
| fixed-moment exponent `p Delta` | explicit-formula growth asymptotics |
| Type-II or scalar-prime fixed saving | arithmetic correlation estimates |
| pole/critical-scale comparison | Mellin asymptotics with exact constants |
| shifted-de Branges domain separation | gamma decay and Paley--Wiener theory |
| exclusion of an almost-equally-deep collateral pair | zeta-specific local divisor theorem |
| compact-local determinant convergence to `xi` | tightness, domain control, and analytic identification |
| `Theta=1` or a uniform strip | an actual theorem about zeta zeros |

Ordinary category theory remembers composition, equivalence, universal
properties, and exactness.  The current obstruction lives in an order and at
a scale.  Even an enriched categorical formulation must import the norms,
rates, and arithmetic estimates; it cannot manufacture them.

## 9. Counterexamples every categorical synthesis must survive

Any proposed umbrella statement should be rejected unless it handles all of
the following explicitly.

1. **Witt collapse:** the one-positive/one-negative mirror plane is
   Witt-trivial but indefinite.
2. **Total probes without positivity:** the matrix (3.5) is positive on all
   prescribed local/modulated packets and negative on a coherent sum.
3. **Positive nonconservative averaging:** dephasing (4.8) turns an indefinite
   mirror into a positive diagonal.
4. **Contractible trace glue:** arbitrary even/odd duplicated bridge geometry
   has zero supertrace for every word.
5. **Duality without polarization:** (6.4) has exact reflected duality off the
   critical line.
6. **Filtered margins with no gap:** compatible positive stages may have
   lower edges tending to zero.
7. **Domain mismatch:** an exact detector in one analytic category need not
   admit any nonzero intertwiner from the certified compact-support category.

These examples rule out the slogans “positivity is categorical,” “local
positivity is a sheaf,” “naturality preserves the sign,” “the trace determines
the metric,” and “a duality pairing is a positive polarization.”

## 11. Recommended canonical formulation

The strongest honest headline is:

> The completed zeta program is an order-enriched diagram, not merely an
> identity in a category.  Local probes fail unless they generate the whole
> positive cone; conservative functors retain the negative index; positive
> mixtures cannot cancel an aligned carrier without a transverse state; and
> trace decategorification cannot supply a polarization.  Therefore the
> remaining mathematical content is a zeta-specific transverse order theorem
> or an independently generated global polarization, together with a closed
> quantitative limit.

Avoid stronger claims such as “category theory proves all witness escapes
impossible” or “the obstruction is a categorical invariant.”  The carrier
size and the actual-prime/collateral angle are not invariants of the bare
category.  A new arithmetic functor with genuinely additional ordered
structure could escape all four lemmas; that is precisely what a breakthrough
would have to construct.
