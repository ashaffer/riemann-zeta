It repairs the outer Mellin phase, removes every surviving promotion of the
R116 balanced-semiprime packet to a global reduction, separates fixed and
varying detector quantifiers, and removes the R123 adjacent-filter fork as a
purportedly smaller target.

After those repairs, no fixed-power estimate remains hidden in the chain.
The faithful arithmetic endpoint is still

\[
 O_{\rm full}=E_I^Q\le X^{1-\kappa+o(1)},\qquad0<\kappa\le1, \tag{0.1}

on every block of one licensed fixed-window regular partition.  By R180,
(0.1) is exponent-equivalent to a zero-free strip of width
`eta=kappa/2` in this relevant range.  Neither (0.1) nor the strip has been
proved.

What did come out is a disposition of three terminal constructions actually
tested in the R105--R130 architecture.  The earlier version called this an
exhaustive trilemma; that was not proved.  Iterated filters, proper
subfamilies, and arbitrary linear combinations were never classified.

## 2. Disposition of three tested terminal routes

### Proposition 2.1 (tested endpoints; not an exhaustion theorem)

Consider the following three terminal constructions obtained from the
complete R105/R71 field:

1. restriction to the R116 primitive balanced-semiprime packet;
2. restoration by R128 complete finite chirp duality; and
3. R123 truncated-Mobius cofactor collapse with the R125 adjacent detector
   pair.

Their proved statuses are as follows.

1. **Local restriction:** the route ends at an isolated primitive packet.
   It has an exact Hermitian signed-coefficient Mellin representation and a
   rigorously positive narrow band, but no proved order-reflecting map to the
   complete positive energy.  Full-shell sign change is presently only a
   D-rated floating diagnostic.
2. **Faithful restoration:** every omitted conductor, gcd, profile,
   orientation, and Vaughan member is restored.  The output is exactly
   `O_full=E_I^Q`.
3. **Filtered faithfulness:** the free cofactor is collapsed and two adjacent
   filters are used to retain zero response.  On the collapsed tail they
   coincide, while their independent direction is

   \[
     C_{P-1}-C_P=P^{-1/2}\tau_{-\log P},               \tag{2.1}
   \]

   applied to the complete original field.  The output therefore contains a
   scale-rescaled copy of the endpoint.

Consequently none of these three executed terminal constructions supplies a
proved faithful positive target strictly smaller than the original endpoint.
This says nothing exhaustive about their arbitrary compositions, proper
subfamilies, new linear combinations, or iterated filter banks.

#### Proof

R120 proves the exact signed-coefficient representation, while R124 proves
the positive narrow band and records only numerical full-shell sign change.
Neither report proves an isolated-packet order-reflecting inequality.  R128 proves, after
complete recombination,

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 O_{\rm full}
  &=\sum_{q_1,q_2}C(q_1)C(q_2)
       \sum_{m_1,m_2\ge1}L(q_1m_1,q_2m_2)\\
  &=\int\psi(R)\left|\sum_q C(q)\sum_m f_R(qm)\right|^2dR
   =E_I^Q,                                             \tag{2.2}
 \end{aligned}
\]

where `C=mu*Lambda=-mu log`; this is alternative 2.  R125 proves both
`C_(P-1)T=C_PT` on the collapsed tail and (2.1) on the complete field,
which is alternative 3 for the retained adjacent pair.  These identities
establish the three statuses above.  QED.

The former exhaustion claim has a simple warning example.  For adjacent
multipliers `A_(P-1),A_P` with no common zero, a scalar row
`C_(P-1)+z C_P` fails at any one zeta zero for at most one value of `z`.
Avoiding the countable forbidden set gives qualitative zero-faithfulness,
yet this baseline-plus-shift mixture is not literally one of the three
terminal cases above.  This supplies no quantitative lower frame and no
strip, but it shows why an actual classification theorem would need a
defined operator class and uniform inverse-norm hypothesis.

This is a synthesis of proved project identities, not a literature-novelty
claim and not a no-go theorem for other mathematics.

## 3. Correct exponent ledger

For the complete fixed-window energy, (0.1) gives

\[
 \Re\rho\le1-\frac\kappa2.                             \tag{3.1}
\]

For the R123/R125 conditional rescaling ledger, let

\[
 a=a_k=\frac1{2(k+1)},\qquad P=X^{a+o(1)}.             \tag{3.2}
\]

If **both complete filtered fields** had energy at most
`X^(1-kappa+o(1))`, then (2.1) gives

\[
 E_I^Q(X/P)\le P X^{1-\kappa+o(1)}.                    \tag{3.3}
\]

The resulting sharp strip ledger is

\[
 \eta=\frac{\kappa/2-a}{1-a},                         \tag{3.4}
\]

and the safe weaker ledger is `eta>=kappa/2-a`.  Both require

\[
 \kappa>2a=\frac1{k+1}.                               \tag{3.5}
\]

Equations (3.3)--(3.5) are correct, but Proposition 2.1 shows why they do not
define a smaller arithmetic target: the faithful direction in (3.3) is the
complete field itself at the smaller scale.

\[

## 6. What survives

There is one clean optional adapter question:

> For a predeclared locally constant fixed-total-width schedule
> `k(R)->infinity`, `k(R)log k(R)=o(R)`, does an every-block bound
> `E_(ell,k(R))<=exp((1-2eta+o(1))R)`, `0<eta<=1/2`, force
> `Re(rho)<=1-eta`?

The pointwise multiplier converges to a nonzero causal-box multiplier at
every off-line zero, so the statement is plausible.  Pointwise convergence
alone is not a proof: one needs a subexponential lower-frame/coefficient-
extraction estimate for the changing infinite zero spectrum.  R80 proves a
related theorem only for its proportional-order dyadic bank.

Even a proof would close an adapter, not the arithmetic gap.  The required
complete growing-order fixed-power estimate would remain strip-strength, and
R125 would still make the adjacent-filter route a rescaled endpoint.

Any next S1 proposal must therefore begin with either a concrete finite
pole--archimedean--prime identity or a genuinely non-tautological two-scale
structural norm/one-sided law, together with a strict adapter coupling the
actual coefficients to the reflection-odd carrier.  Neither slogan is yet a
theorem statement.  If no such statement survives admission, the decision path
falls back to the precommitted QP source-fiber bifurcation.
