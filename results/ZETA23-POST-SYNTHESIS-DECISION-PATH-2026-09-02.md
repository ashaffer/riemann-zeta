## 3. The corrected arithmetic endpoint

R116 power-saves every proper additive conductor only on its balanced
squarefree-semiprime packet and exactly rotates that packet's primitive
reciprocal phase into an ordinary product phase.  The literal packet carries
the outer Mellin phase

\[
 h(c)c^{1-it}=A_{U,V}(c)c^{-it},                     \tag{3.1}
\]

not merely `h(c)c`.  It is one isolated member of the already squared R105
energy with a signed-coefficient representation; no order-reflecting
positive-energy implication is proved for it.

R128 gives the global statement.  Restore every conductor, gcd sector,
fixed-ratio profile, conjugate orientation, and tail/head packet, and use

\[
 C(n)=(\mu*\Lambda)(n)=-\mu(n)\log n.                \tag{3.2}
\]

The resulting complete ordinary dual is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 O_{\rm full}
 &=\sum_{q_1,q_2}C(q_1)C(q_2)
     \sum_{m_1,m_2\ge1}L(q_1m_1,q_2m_2)\\
 &=E^Q_I,
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{3.3}
\]

the complete `Q_h`-transformed R71 energy.  This identity has no conductor
remainder, but it is an exact coordinate change back to the calibrated
endpoint.  An isolated `g=1` profile has the signed form

\[
 \sum_{(c,n)=1}C(c)C(n)c^{-it}n^{-1-it}
       \Phi_\lambda(n/c),                            \tag{3.4}
\]

and must not be substituted for (3.3).

The desired milestone is therefore the complete-family bound

\[
 O_{\rm full}=E^Q_I\le X^{1-\kappa+o(1)},
 \qquad 0<\kappa\le1,                               \tag{3.5}
\]

on one fixed window and every block of one licensed regular partition.  The
fixed-`h` inverse of `Q_h` preserves this exponent, so in this range the
complete-energy correspondence remains `kappa=2 eta`.

The former R123 cofactor-collapsed fork is not a smaller target.  At
the favorable fixed-order cutoff its cofactor exponent is
`a_k=1/[2(k+1)]`.  A collapsed-energy saving gives only

\[
 \eta\ge \frac\kappa2-a_k                            \tag{3.6}
\]

in the safe recorded ledger, and hence requires
`kappa>1/(k+1)`.  R125 proves more decisively that the adjacent filters are
identical on the collapsed tail and that their only independent direction is
`P^{-1/2} tau_{-log P}` applied to the complete original field.  Hence the
display is merely the exponent ledger for a scale-rescaled endpoint.
Sending `k -> infinity` moves to one of two sublinear single-schedule
families: fixed total width `h=ell/k`, `k log k=o(R)`, or fixed step `h`,
`k^2=o(R)`.  Neither has the needed varying-test converse.  R80's proportional-order
dyadic bank has a different moving-edge converse and does not license this
collapse.

Equation (3.5) is strip-equivalent and is not advertised as an easier
intermediate lemma.  It now serves as an admission test: a proposed new
mechanism must act on the complete family and produce an inequality not
equivalent merely by expanding (3.3).

\[
 C_{a,b}=T_{a,b}A_a                                  \tag{7.1}

Because this lane targets RH rather than the immediate strip, it is a bounded

## 9. Recursive control policy

Every active proposal carries the debt vector

```text
(mask, source, coefficient sign, normalization, quantifiers,
 fixed window, resolution, exponent, recompletion, downstream adapter).
```

Classify debts as:

- **red:** a new fixed-power actual-prime estimate, exact universal
  cancellation, or uncontrolled global uniformity;
- **amber:** imported uniformity, degeneracy, or a finite-to-continuum
  transfer requiring an audit;
- **green:** exact algebra, formal proof, or correctly scoped replay.

An active sprint may contain only one red debt.  Two red debts in series mean
the route must be decomposed or parked.

An experiment is admissible only if every material outcome does at least one
of the following:

1. discharges a named red debt;
2. kills a named proof class with a passport-complete countermodel;
3. closes a first-open adapter; or
4. yields an independently publishable theorem.

Update the decision tree only after an exact theorem, a faithful
countermodel, an adapter closure, or new literature.  Then canonicalize the
claim, propagate it through the dependency graph once, rerun the hostile
models, and rerank.  Park a branch after two consecutive bounded sprints
with no theorem-status or debt change; reopen it only for a new arithmetic
invariant or interface, never for new notation.
