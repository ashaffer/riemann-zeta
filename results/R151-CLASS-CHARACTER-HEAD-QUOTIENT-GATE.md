# R151 class-character head-quotient gate

## Status

The cubic exterior mask of R147 can be reformulated entirely in the class
group of its quadratic resolvent.  That reformulation produces a genuine new
bank of exact positive degree-zero detectors, not just copies of the three
`S_3` characters.  It also identifies the exceptional-field loophole with a
precise least-prime problem.

Let `F=Q(sqrt(D))` be real quadratic and let `Cl(F)` be its ordinary ideal
class group.  For a nontrivial character `eta` of `Cl(F)`, put

```text
Phi_eta=Ind_F^Q(1)-Ind_F^Q(eta)
       =1+chi_D-Ind_F^Q(eta),                               (0.1)

F_eta(s)=L(s,Phi_eta)=zeta_F(s)/L_F(s,eta).                 (0.2)
```

Then:

1. `Phi_eta` has degree zero, trivial multiplicity one, nonnegative
   logarithmic Euler coefficients at every rational prime power, and exact
   signed conductor and Gamma cancellation.
2. At rational primes inert in `F`, its complete local factor is one
   automatically.  At a split prime `p=P Pbar`, its coefficients vanish at
   every power exactly when the class of `P` is killed by `eta`.  Ramified
   head primes impose the same condition on their order-two ideal classes.
3. If `Gamma_H` is generated by those split and ramified prime ideals over
   `p<=H`, every nontrivial character of

   ```text
   C_H=Cl(F)/Gamma_H                                      (0.3)
   ```

   is an exact head-deleting detector.  Thus the available bank is
   `C_H^`.
4. For a squarefree-discriminant totally real `S_3` cubic field, its standard
   Artin character is induced from an order-three character `theta` of
   `Cl(F)`, and “no inert rational prime through `H`” is exactly
   `theta in C_H^`.
5. Minkowski gives only `P(theta)<=sqrt(D)/2`.  Under GRH, current class-group
   generator theorems give `P(theta)<=4.01(log D)^2` and slightly sharper
   asymptotic constants.  The GRH bound is incompatible with the R147
   sub-square-root high-jet budget; no unconditional polylogarithmic theorem
   of this kind is known.
6. A Hecke zero-density estimate can select a denominator-zero-free member
   when `|C_H|` is sufficiently large.  In the smallest case `C_H=C_3`, the
   two nontrivial characters have the same `L`-function and there is no
   amplification.
7. Averaging the whole bank gives an exact unramified-class-field quotient.
   Its normalized absolute conductor/divisor ledger remains
   `(2+o(1))log D`, not `log D/|C_H|`.  Class-group orthogonality therefore
   does not repair the known `log D asymp H` family scale.

This converts the vague phrase “exceptional subentropy cubic field” into an
explicit conjunction: an unusually late prime detecting an unramified class
character, a sufficiently large residual head quotient or individual
denominator nonvanishing, and the same `o(H^kappa)` absolute ledger with
`kappa<1/2`.  None of these results proves either side of the zeta fixed-strip
dichotomy.

```text
class-character positive degree-zero detector              THEOREM
complete inert-prime local deletion                         THEOREM
head deletion iff character factors through C_H             THEOREM
cubic no-inert condition as least class nonresidue          THEOREM
unconditional P(eta)<=sqrt(D)/2                             THEOREM
GRH P(eta)<<log^2 D                                         IMPORTED
unconditional P(eta)<<log^(2+o(1)) D                        OPEN
large-bank zero-free denominator selection                 CONDITIONAL THEOREM
bank-average divisor cost log D/|C_H|                       FALSE
rank-one exceptional configuration C_H=C_3                 OPEN
fixed uniform zeta zero-free strip                          NOT PROVED
zeros approaching one                                       NOT PROVED
```

## 1. The squarefree cubic field is an unramified class character

Let `K/Q` be a totally real `S_3` cubic field of positive odd squarefree
discriminant `D`.  Let `E` be its Galois closure and

```text
F=Q(sqrt(D)).                                                (1.1)
```

Then `Gal(E/F)=C_3`.  The discriminant formula gives

```text
D_E=D^3=D_F^3 N_(F/Q)(d_(E/F)),                             (1.2)
```

so the relative discriminant is one.  All embeddings are real.  Hence
`E/F` is an everywhere-unramified, totally real cyclic cubic extension,
cut out by an order-three character

```text
theta:Cl(F)->mu_3.                                          (1.3)
```

Use the ordinary, or wide, class group here.  Its characters are trivial at
the two real places.  Narrow-class characters can carry infinity type and
would change the Gamma ledger.

Artin induction gives

```text
Std_K=Ind_F^Q(theta),
L(s,Std_K)=L_F(s,theta)=L_F(s,theta^(-1)),                  (1.4)

zeta_F(s)=zeta(s)L(s,chi_D).                                (1.5)
```

For `p` not dividing `D`:

- if `p` is inert in `F`, its prime ideal is `(p)`, hence principal and
  `theta((p))=1`; its `S_3` Frobenius is a transposition;
- if `p=P Pbar` splits in `F`, then `theta(P)=1` is the identity class,
  while `theta(P)!=1` is a three-cycle and `p` is inert in `K`.

If `p` divides `D`, the ramified prime ideal satisfies `[P]^2=1`, so the
odd-order character `theta` is trivial on it.  Therefore, with

```text
P(theta)=min{N P: theta(P)!=1},                             (1.6)
```

one has the exact equality

```text
P(theta)=min{p:p is inert in K}.                            (1.7)
```

The left side is a least class-character nonresidue, not an ordinary
Dirichlet least nonresidue.

## 2. The general unramified class-character detector

Now let `eta` be any nontrivial character of `Cl(F)`, of arbitrary order.
Define `Phi_eta` and `F_eta` by (0.1)--(0.2).  Since

```text
Ind_F^Q(1)=1+chi_D,                                        (2.1)
```

`Phi_eta` has virtual degree zero and contains the trivial representation
once.  Both induced representations are even, two-dimensional, and have
Artin conductor `D`; hence signed finite conductor and signed Gamma factors
cancel exactly.

Let `T=p^(-s)`.  The logarithmic coefficient divided by `log p` is as
follows.

### 2.1 Split primes

If `p=P Pbar`, then `[Pbar]=[P]^(-1)`.  Write `a=eta(P)`.  The local factor is

```text
F_(eta,p)(s)=[(1-aT)(1-a^(-1)T)]/(1-T)^2,                  (2.2)
```

and at the `v`th prime power the coefficient is

```text
2-a^v-a^(-v)=2[1-Re(a^v)]>=0.                              (2.3)
```

It vanishes for every `v` if and only if `a=1`.

### 2.2 Inert primes

If `p` is inert, the prime ideal `(p)` is principal and has norm `p^2`.
The numerator and denominator local factors agree:

```text
F_(eta,p)(s)=1                                              (2.4)
```

at every power.  This automatic deletion is the advantage over a rational
higher-order twist.

### 2.3 Ramified primes

If `p` divides `D`, the unique prime `P` has `[P]^2=1`, so `eta(P)` is
`+1` or `-1`.  The local factor is

```text
F_(eta,p)(s)=(1-eta(P)T)/(1-T),                             (2.5)
```

with coefficient

```text
1-eta(P)^v in {0,2}.                                       (2.6)
```

It is again nonnegative, and complete deletion requires `eta(P)=1`.

Equations (2.3), (2.4), and (2.6) prove prime-power positivity globally;
this is stronger than positivity only at the first prime power.

## 3. The exact head quotient

Let `Gamma_H` be the subgroup of `Cl(F)` generated by

```text
[P] for P over a split rational p<=H,
and [P] for p|D, p<=H.                                     (3.1)
```

Equivalently one may generate with every prime ideal of norm at most `H`;
the extra inert ideals are principal.  Put

```text
C_H=Cl(F)/Gamma_H,
B_H=C_H^.                                                   (3.2)
```

### Theorem 3.1 -- exact class-character head deletion

For a nontrivial ordinary class character `eta`, the logarithmic derivative
`-F_eta'/F_eta` has no coefficient supported on a rational prime `p<=H` if
and only if

```text
eta in B_H.                                                 (3.3)
```

The forward implication follows already from the `v=1` cases of (2.3) and
(2.6); the reverse implication follows at all powers from Section 2.

For the cubic character `theta` in Section 1,

```text
K has no inert p<=H iff theta in B_H.                       (3.4)
```

Thus every nontrivial member of `B_H`, not only the original cubic
character, supplies an exact positive degree-zero head-deleting quotient.

## 4. Conditional high-jet closure

Fix a hypothetical zeta zero

```text
rho=1-delta+i gamma,
0<delta<eta_0.                                              (4.1)
```

Choose `r,R,lambda` as in R147:

```text
z_*=1+r+i gamma,
d=r+delta<R<r+eta_0,
I(lambda)>log(d/r),                                         (4.2)

H_k=exp(lambda k/r),
kappa=(r/lambda)log(R/d)<eta_0<1/2.                         (4.3)
```

Suppose that for the selected Turan orders `k` there are real quadratic
fields `F_k` and nontrivial `eta_k in B_(H_k)` such that

1. `L_(F_k)(s,eta_k)` is zero-free on `|s-z_*|<=R`; and
2.

   ```text
   log D_(F_k)+log(|gamma|+3)=o(H_k^kappa).                 (4.4)
   ```

Then `rho` cannot exist.

The proof is the R147 high-jet theorem verbatim.  The Euler logarithm is
nonnegative and its complete head through `H_k` vanishes by Theorem 3.1.
The zeta factor in `zeta_F=zeta L(chi_D)` supplies the target zero, while
the other numerator factor cannot cancel it.  The denominator is nonzero in
the localization disk.  Finally the absolute completed-divisor count for
the numerator and denominator is

```text
O(log D_F+log(|gamma|+3)).                                  (4.5)
```

Signed degree, conductor, and Gamma cancellation do not remove (4.5).
Condition (4.4) is exactly what makes the outer divisor smaller than the
Cauchy localization reserve.

For a proof by contradiction from one fixed zero, `gamma` is fixed while
`k` grows.  It is nevertheless useful to retain the height term in the
uniform statement.

## 5. How exceptional must the class character be?

The quotient `C_H` is nontrivial exactly when there is a class character
with `P(eta)>H`.

### 5.1 Unconditional Minkowski bound

Every ideal class in a real quadratic field has an integral representative
of norm at most `sqrt(D)/2`.  Choose a class on which `eta` is nontrivial and
factor such a representative into prime ideals.  At least one factor is
detected by `eta`.  Therefore

```text
P(eta)<=sqrt(D)/2.                                          (5.1)
```

In particular, `P(eta)>H` implies

```text
D>4H^2.                                                     (5.2)
```

This is a real lower bound, but it controls `D`, not `log D`, and is fully
compatible with (4.4).

### 5.2 GRH-scale generator bound

Under GRH for the relevant Hecke `L`-functions, the ordinary class group is
generated by prime ideals of norm at most

```text
4.01(log D)^2.                                              (5.3)
```

Grenie--Molteni also prove `(4+o(1))(log D)^2`, and their later work lowers
the leading constant below four in fixed degree apart from explicit finite
ranges.  Thus GRH implies

```text
P(eta)<=4.01(log D)^2                                      (5.4)
```

for every nontrivial class character.  Equations (5.4) and `P(eta)>H`
force `log D` to be at least a constant times `sqrt(H)`, which contradicts
`log D=o(H^kappa)` for every `kappa<1/2`.

Primary sources: Grenie and Molteni,
[*Explicit bounds for generators of the class group*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.02430),
and
[*Breaking the 4 barrier for the bound of a generating set of the class group*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09461).

Unconditionally, available least-prime and Chebotarev bounds are polynomial
in discriminants, not polylogarithmic.  The sharp route-closing arithmetic
target is

```text
P(eta)<<(log D)^(2+o(1))                                   (5.5)
```

for nontrivial unramified order-three characters of real quadratic fields.
Equation (5.5) is a GRH-scale theorem and is not currently known.  Proving
it would kill the exceptional-field mechanism; proving sufficiently strong
counterexamples, together with denominator nonvanishing, would activate the
conditional closure in Section 4.

## 6. When a large bank selects a zero-free denominator

Let

```text
M=|C_H|.                                                    (6.1)
```

The log-free Hecke zero-density theorem of Thorner--Zaman applies directly
to the characters of `B_H`.  If a target disk is contained in

```text
Re(s)>=1-epsilon,        |Im(s)|<=T,                        (6.2)
```

then, with absolute effective constants,

```text
sum_(eta in B_H) N(1-epsilon,T,eta)
 <<(exp[O(1)]D^2T^2)^(81 epsilon).                          (6.3)
```

For `epsilon<=10^(-3)`, `81` may be replaced by `74`.  This is Theorem 1.1
of Thorner and Zaman,
[*Explicit results on the distribution of zeros of Hecke L-functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.08086),
specialized to a quadratic base field, trivial finite modulus, and the
congruence subgroup defining `C_H`.

Every character whose `L`-function has a zero in the disk contributes at
least one to (6.3).  Hence a nontrivial zero-free member exists whenever

```text
M-1>C(D^2T^2)^(81 epsilon).                                 (6.4)
```

If one insists on a detector different from the original pair
`theta,theta^(-1)`, replace `M-1` by `M-3`.

When `C_H=C_3`, the only nontrivial characters are `theta` and its inverse,
and (1.4) shows that their `L`-functions agree.  There is then no bank
amplification.  Thus a surviving exceptional field can hide in a precise
rank-one configuration: a late order-three class nonresidue but no large
head quotient.

Even (6.4) only supplies local denominator nonvanishing.  Section 4 still
requires the separate absolute scale (4.4).

## 7. Orthogonality packages the ledger but does not shrink it

Let `H_C/F` be the everywhere-unramified totally real class field with
Galois group `C_H`.  The conductor-discriminant formula gives

```text
D_(H_C)=D^M,                                                (7.1)
```

and character factorization gives

```text
zeta_(H_C)(s)=product_(eta in B_H)L_F(s,eta).                (7.2)
```

The geometric mean of all nontrivial detectors is therefore exactly

```text
G_C(s)
 =[product_(eta!=1) F_eta(s)]^(1/(M-1))
 =zeta_F(s)^(M/(M-1))/zeta_(H_C)(s)^(1/(M-1)).              (7.3)
```

At a split prime whose class is `c in C_H`, the averaged logarithmic
coefficient at power `v` is

```text
0                     if c^v=1,
2M/(M-1)              if c^v!=1.                           (7.4)
```

It is positive and deletes the prescribed head.  But the normalized
absolute conductor contribution in (7.3) is

```text
[M/(M-1)]log D+[1/(M-1)]log(D^M)
 =2M/(M-1) log D.                                          (7.5)
```

The height degrees behave the same way.  Thus the bank has cost
`(2+o(1))log D`, never `log D/M`.  Every convex average has total source
weight one and the same order of absolute ledger.

Orthogonality also packages the common-zero obstruction.  The formally
admissible pattern

```text
ord_rho L_F(s,eta)=ord_rho zeta_F(s)
                  for every eta in B_H                     (7.6)
```

makes every detector regular at `rho` and gives

```text
ord_rho zeta_(H_C)=M ord_rho zeta_F.                        (7.7)
```

Standard Dedekind multiplicity bounds are
`O(M[log D+log(|gamma|+3)])`, so they do not exclude (7.7).
This is the class-field form of the regular-character null direction from
R145 and R149.

## 8. Why ordinary external twists are weaker

For comparison, let `xi` be an even rational quadratic character coprime to
`D` and twist the original cubic standard factor.  The virtual character

```text
1+chi_D-Std_K tensor xi                                    (8.1)
```

still has nonnegative prime-power coefficients.  At an unramified power,
with `a=xi(p)^v`, they are

```text
S_3 class          identity       transposition       3-cycle
coefficient        2(1-a)         0                   2+a.  (8.2)
```

Exact head deletion forces `xi(p)=1` at every completely split head prime
and at every ramified head prime.  The only choices automatic from the
original `S_3` extension are `1` and `chi_D`; both reproduce the original
standard representation.  A new rational twist must interpolate many local
signs, has signed conductor `q_xi^(-2)`, and pays absolute ledger

```text
O(log(D q_xi^2)+log T).                                    (8.3)
```

Higher-order rational twists require paired conjugates and impose at least
as many head conditions.  Generic CRT or family entropy costs
`log q_xi` on the order of `H/log H` or worse, above every
`H^kappa`, `kappa<1/2`.  An exceptional cheap twist is another least-
nonresidue problem.

Unramified class characters are therefore the unique clean enlargement
found here: they add no finite conductor at all and delete inert rational
primes automatically.  Their remaining cost is the field discriminant and
their existence is exactly the head-quotient problem.

## 9. Verdict

The cubic exceptional-field branch now has a precise arithmetic statement.
A successful sequence for one target zero must provide arbitrarily large
`H` and triples `(F,eta,H)` such that

```text
eta!=1 and P(eta)>H,
L_F(s,eta) is zero-free in the target disk,
log D_F+log(|gamma|+3)=o(H^kappa),       kappa<1/2.          (9.1)
```

A large `C_H` can solve the middle condition by (6.4), but it does not help
the last condition.  The minimal `C_H=C_3` case evades the bank entirely.
GRH predicts that the first and last conditions are incompatible; current
unconditional theory does not prove that incompatibility and also does not
construct the required exceptional sequence.

The new detector and bank are exact, but they do not establish a zeta
zero-free strip.  They instead reduce the remaining exceptional arithmetic
to the least unramified class-character nonresidue and the rank-one
denominator problem, with no hidden conductor saving from orthogonality.
