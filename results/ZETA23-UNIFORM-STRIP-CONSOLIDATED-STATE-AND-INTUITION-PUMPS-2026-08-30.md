## 0. Bottom line

The project has not proved RH, a fixed zero-free strip, `DPA_P(.019)`,
`LTRAD_P(.0189,.001)`, COSE, PWCT, or the sharp four-cycle bound.

It has learned something more specific than “the problem is hard.”

1. The contemplated small fixed strip near `Re(s)=1` is only an intermediate
   milestone; no amplification from it to RH is present.
2. The current strip route has two independent open locks: a prime-supported
   upper antenna and a source-conditioned lower return.
3. Generic moments, including the hypothetical sharp four-cycle endpoint
   passed through every audited generic conversion, miss the required
   transverse exponent by a large amount.
4. Sparse, bounded-cluster, literal progression, and product/digit
   obstructions have scoped terminators.  The residual candidate class
   includes diffuse, growing-rank, adaptive obstructions; only polynomial
   effective participation is currently forced.
5. Source negativity does not localize an optimizing separator or positive
   antenna.  In PWCT the source interval disappears from the normalized
   problem except through `(t_0,D)`.
6. Close reflected pairs do not carry most of the source's negative mass:
   deleting every pair-incident prime loses only
   `Y^(-17/33+o(1))`.  But the surviving set need not be contiguous, so this
   is not a new legal LTRAD event and pairs may still cancel high-band peaks.
7. Real-node systems can reproduce source contiguity, prime-scale density and
   gaps, positivity, diffuseness, carrier support, and the tested soft moment
   inputs while violating the proposed bound.  A surviving proof must expose
   a deterministic property of the actual prime logarithms.
8. Dualizing, adding an SDP, projecting to a carrier, or naming an inverse
   theorem is not progress unless the new statement has a strictly easier
   hypothesis passport and a complete downstream adapter.

Within the current PWCT/LTRAD branch, the mathematical frontier is therefore
not “find a cleverer fourth moment.”  It is an actual-prime,
coefficient-sensitive, adaptive convex-hull/covariance theorem.  The signed
upper gate and genuinely different strip architectures remain parallel
possibilities.

### 1.2 QP/Turan fixed-strip architecture

At a half-integer center `Y asymp N`, put

```text
H_Y=[Y^.01,Y^(50/33)].
```

The authoritative conditional chain is

```text
prime-supported DPA_P(.019)
        +
source-conditioned LTRAD_P(.0189,.001)
        |
        v
no mass-normalized prime event of depth N^-.001
        |
        v
Turan/phase-localization adapter
        |
        v
fixed zero-free width 10^-6.                         (1.1)
```

Here `DPA_P(.019)` permits **real signed coefficients**:

```text
sum_p y_p=1,
inf_(t in H_Y)sum_p y_p cos(tu_p)>=-Y^-.019.         (1.2)
```

It gives the radial upper bound `r_P(H_Y)<=Y^-.019`.
`LTRAD_P(.0189,.001)` says that a legal negative prime event gives
`r_P(H_Y)>=Y^(-.0189+o(1))`.  The strict exponent ordering contradicts the
event.

This upper gate is genuinely prime-only.  If `r_full` is the radius allowing
all prime powers, then

```text
r_full<=r_P.                                         (1.3)
```

Thus a small full prime-power radius does not imply a small prime-only
radius.  Conversely, a zero-free strip can supply a nonnegative
prime-supported antenna, but that is the strip-to-DPA direction and cannot be
used to prove the strip.

For an event of mean depth `D>=Y^(-.001+o(1))`, a sufficient transverse
target for the recorded one-atom mixing construction is

```text
s_v=inf_(y:y.v=-1) sup_(t in H_Y)y.a_P(t)
    >=Y^(-.0179+o(1)),
v=a_P(t_0)+Dq_P.                                    (1.4)
```

The exact mixing inequality is

```text
r_P(H_Y)>=D s_v/(1+s_v),                             (1.5)
```

so multiplication by `D` produces the radial exponent `.0189`.

Both locks in `(1.1)` are open.  Solving only the lower-return problem still
leaves `DPA_P`; solving only `DPA_P` still leaves `LTRAD_P`.  An
event-conditioned common certificate could be weaker than this universal
split, but no such coupling theorem is known.

Finally, for the contemplated width,

```text
zero-free width 10^-6 near Re(s)=1 != RH.             (1.6)
```

The repository has no proved amplifier from width `10^-6` to the critical
line.  This is not the absurdly broad claim that every possible constant
zero-free half-strip would fall short: reaching `Re(s)>1/2`, together with
functional-equation symmetry, would settle RH.

## 3. Results that changed the decision tree

### 3.1 The sharp four-cycle bound is not the strip gate

The sharp four-cycle problem remains open.  More importantly, even its
hypothetical endpoint passed through the audited generic moment conversion
would give only

```text
s_v >>Y^(-41/66-o(1))=Y^(-.621212...-o(1)),          (3.1)
```

where `(1.4)` needs `Y^(-.0179+o(1))`.  The exponent miss is about
`.603312`.  The theorem could matter only inside a new inverse theorem that
also preserves source normalization, the prime mask, coefficient sign, and
the global one-sided floor.  That qualification concerns the LTRAD edge: FC
could still matter independently to the QP-upper/`DPA_P` problem.  It is not
a direct strip dependency under the presently audited adapters.

The durable FC reductions should not be confused with a proof.  Complex
coefficients reduce losslessly to nonnegative coefficients; literal FC is
equivalent up to logarithms to restricted type `RT`; the exact remaining
cross-defect term is `XDC`; and proper-prime-power coefficient colors are
removable.  Earlier factorial multiplicity targets are strictly stronger
than literal FC and must not be silently reinstated.

### 3.2 Local affine compression was selected before excess existed

The A2 serializer was necessary and is useful.  Its literal actual-prime
fixture, however, is almost entirely singleton at the exact-secant level.
Natural affine-line classes retain only about `1.071%` of the factorial
atoms.  Allowing two-point “rich lines” captures everything but performs no
compression.  Thus unconditional affine compression is false, while the
conditional inverse statement still needs a certified A2 excess.  A4
passing on a sparse fixture is bookkeeping validation, not evidence for the
inverse theorem.

### 3.3 Primitive shifted cubes supplied structure, not aggregation

The primitive shifted-cube route removed gcd and parametrization mirages and
proved useful local facts: at most one point for fixed `u`, equal-`A`
spacing, a six-point span `>>P^(39/80)`, and a forced continued-fraction
partial quotient `>>P^2`.  It did not reach the target aggregate exponent
`N_prim(P)<<P^(9/16+epsilon)`.  The strongest fully licensed uniform bound in
the record is `P^(17/24+epsilon)`; the sometimes mentioned `P^(5/8+epsilon)`
depends on an unchecked Huxley-IV application and is not unconditional.  Its
off-diagonal pair-energy gate is another open theorem, not a finished
replacement.  It should remain a scoped number-theory side branch unless a
direct adapter back to an open strip edge is exhibited.

### 3.4 Near saturation does not imply low entropy

A diffuse coisometry can exactly saturate the abstract frame budget.  An
arbitrary Sidon seed can generate an exact Fejer-type autocorrelation without
digit factors or a long progression.  Therefore

```text
near Bessel equality -> digit tensor
```

is false without a new stability/classification theorem.  Tensor `SR2PF`
closes the product/digit branch only after an upstream theorem actually
produces all compatible tensor cuts and labels.

### 3.5 Carrier projection and SDP reformulations did not reduce LTRAD

- On pair-free shells, actual-prime COSE is exactly full LTRAD.
- The abstract source/carrier relaxation of COSE has a legal Fejer
  countermodel.
- SCPSI is equivalent to exponent-level LTRAD relative to the known energy
  upper bound.
- APDIE, as proposed, is ill-typed and has no valid adapter.
- A paired `q/v` KKT saddle has no generic contact overlap; abstract convex
  systems can assign the two radial problems arbitrary values and disjoint
  contact measures.

These are valuable equivalence collapses.  They prevent us from counting a
new notation or dual program as a new theorem.

### 3.6 PWCT falsified the source-overlap intuition

For a positive carrier probability `alpha`, define

```text
Phi_alpha(t)=sum_p alpha_p cos(tu_p),
A(alpha)=D+Phi_alpha(t_0),
a(alpha)=-inf_(t in H_Y)Phi_alpha(t).
```

Then exactly

```text
rho_+(v)=inf_(alpha>=0, alpha in C_Y, sum alpha=1, A(alpha)>0)
          a(alpha)/A(alpha).                         (3.2)
```

The source interval `I` has vanished except through `(t_0,D)`.  Its uniform
probability satisfies `A(lambda_I)=0`, so it lies on the normalization
boundary rather than furnishing a feasible witness.  A failure of PWCT with
proposed radius `epsilon` must obey

```text
A(alpha)>D/(1+epsilon),
P_eff(alpha)>>(epsilon A(alpha))^(-1),
a(alpha)<epsilon A(alpha).                          (3.3)
```

Finite optimizers often used little source mass, and four of ten tested
source-conditioned instances used zero paired mass.  The lesson is not that
actual PWCT is false.  It is that “the source should tether the optimizer” is
not an admissible step without a quantitative overlap theorem.

### 3.7 Reflected pairs are small at the source, not necessarily aloft

For a target-scale separator, the antisymmetric reflected-pair corrector
`y^-` satisfies

```text
|F_(y^-)(t_0)|<<epsilon_tr Y^(-1/33+o(1)).           (3.4)
```

So pairs cannot supply the needed source-time leverage.  But the current
high-band estimate still permits
`E_Y(y^-)^(1/2)<<Y^(.224524+o(1))`.  This is the precise form of the warning:
small source leverage does not imply small cancellation throughout `H_Y`.

### 3.8 The separator hierarchy is one-way

For a fixed legal event, the admissible separator classes obey

```text
all signed LTRAD separators
        superseteq signed carrier COSE separators
        superseteq nonpositive carrier PWCT separators.   (3.5)
```

Consequently their optimal radii satisfy, with the normalizations of their
source reports,

```text
s_v <= s_car <= rho_+.                               (3.6)
```

Thus PWCT can close only the nonpositive-carrier subclass, and COSE can close
only the carrier subclass.  A PWCT counterexample would also refute the
corresponding COSE/LTRAD lower assertion; a PWCT proof does not reverse either
inclusion.  On pair-free shells COSE is LTRAD, so no reduction remains there.

### 3.9 R87 contact matching is closed as a route

The quadratic-discriminant match does not type-check.  The
Blomer--Pascadi square locus has zero coefficient on the single axis and
produces a paired `L2` autocorrelation, whereas R87 requires linear/`L1`
contact.  Reduction Registry R89 records this obstruction.  Reopening the
route requires a new adapter, not another parameter match.

## 5. The errors, ordered by height in the decision tree

### Error 1: treating the contemplated small strip as if it completed RH

This occurred at the root.  Width `10^-6` near `Re(s)=1` is a legitimate
milestone, but it leaves an unbuilt amplification step to `Re(s)=1/2`.

### Error 2: selecting four-cycle mathematics before writing the exponent
adapter

The desired local estimate looked sharp, but the global conversion loses
about `.603312` in exponent.  This should have been calculated before
substantial priority was assigned to the four-cycle endpoint.

### Error 3: optimizing one branch of an AND gate

`DPA_P+LTRAD_P` is a two-lock door.  Progress on transverse return was
repeatedly described without charging the still-open upper antenna.

### Error 4: assuming structural extraction is hereditary

Localization, BSG, packetization, and tensorization can lose source mass,
one-sided floor, weights, prime labels, or `B^-1` precision.  A structure
theorem without these fields cannot enter a downstream terminator.

### Error 5: classifying equality too narrowly

Near saturation was repeatedly pictured as a digit box or a literal Fejer
progression.  Diffuse coisometries and arbitrary Sidon autocorrelations are
equally compatible with the abstract data.

### Error 6: assigning intent to the optimizer

The optimizer was imagined to inherit the source interval or reflected
pairs.  It is an adversary: it may avoid `I`, ignore pairs, spread over the
full shell, and choose coefficients after seeing the event.

### Error 7: confusing a sufficient subclass with the full route

Diffuse positive `PDPA_+` carrierizes cheaply, but is only sufficient.
Failure of the diffuse atom cap would leave sparse/multiblock positive
carrier antennas alive.

### Error 8: letting finite and synthetic evidence change theorem status

Synthetic models refute proof classes, not actual-prime assertions.  Finite
LPs reveal active constraints and optimizer morphology, not asymptotic
exponents—especially when the sampled source events are not asymptotically
legal.

## 6. Adversarial intuition pumps

These are mandatory reasoning devices, not motivational metaphors.

### Pump A: the theorem passport

Every proposed statement carries

```text
(mask, source, coefficient cone, normalization, window/floor,
 quantifier order, resolution, rate, feasibility,
 downstream socket).                                (6.1)
```

If a proposed intermediate has the target's passport, it is the target
renamed.  If a field changes, record the adapter needed to restore it.

For PWCT the passport includes actual primes, `(I,t_0,D,e)`,
`alpha>=0 intersect C_Y`, probability normalization, the entire band, the
`forall event forall alpha exists t` order, exponent `.0179`, and the output
socket `y=-w<=0`.  “Source-conditioned positive prime Turan” has essentially
the same passport and earns no reduction credit.

### Pump B: the three-lock safe

Keep source, mask, and floor separate.

- Source: one designated block is negative at one low time.
- Mask: frequencies are exactly `|log(p/Y)|` for ordinary primes.
- Floor: every adaptive vector must dip somewhere on all of `H_Y`.

Opening two locks says nothing about the third.  Mandatory question:

> Where do the actual indices of `I` re-enter the inequality?

If they do not, source contiguity is not being used.

### Pump C: the hostile optimizer

Assume the optimizer

```text
avoids the source;
uses the full available dimension;
concentrates on any unprotected exceptional set;
chooses coefficients after seeing every fixed construction.
```

Never say “it should resemble the source” or “it should use the pairs” without
a quantitative overlap or stability theorem.

Toy reminder: the source probability has `A(lambda_I)=0`; a disjoint
probability may have `A(alpha)asymp1`.

### Pump D: the empty-chair alarm

A lower bound on an infimum over an empty feasible set is true and useless.
For PWCT feasibility is exactly `max_g V_g>0`.  Before using a radius bound
downstream, exhibit the feasible witness supplied by the upstream theorem.

### Pump E: the quantifier game

Say the order aloud:

```text
for every legal event E,
  for every adaptive alpha with A_E(alpha)>0,
    there exists a height t in H_Y producing the dip. (6.2)
```

A fixed source height or a coefficient vector chosen before `alpha` does not
win this game.  Any minimax interchange must be justified, not narrated.

### Pump F: the Fejer smoke alarm

Test every positivity or moment argument on

```text
K_m(theta)=m^(-1)|sum_(j<m)e^(ijtheta)|^2,
W_m=(K_m-1)/(m-1),
inf W_m=-1/(m-1).                                   (6.3)
```

Then test the smooth prime-density pseudo-node model.  If the proof applies
unchanged, it cannot prove PWCT.  Identify the exact line where actual-prime
arithmetic destroys the model.

### Pump G: the mask-swap ladder

Run hostile models in increasing fidelity:

```text
abstract convex hull
 -> Fejer/half-period lattice
 -> prime-density real nodes with zero-weight filler
 -> finite actual primes
 -> asymptotic actual primes.                       (6.4)
```

Each level licenses only its own conclusion.  Fill the entire shell before
claiming a synthetic model has prime-scale global density or gaps.

### Pump H: the participation speedometer

The current moment engine says only

```text
a(alpha)>>1/P_eff(alpha).                            (6.5)
```

It closes a sparse corner; it does not approach a diffuse optimizer.  No
further moment work earns priority unless it proves either

```text
shallow floor -> near-extremal moment structure,
```

or an upper bound on `P_eff` that reaches the target scale.

### Pump I: the resolution microscope

The source height `t_0<=Y` resolves log frequencies only to `Y^-1`.  The top
band `B=Y^(50/33)` resolves them to

```text
B^-1=Y^(-50/33).
```

The scale ratio is `B^-1/Y^-1=Y^(-17/33)`, equivalently the top band requires
a refinement factor `Y^(17/33)` beyond source-time resolution.  Every
source-to-shadow transport must pay for this superresolution explicitly.
“Nearby primes” at physical scale do not imply nearby Fourier phases at the
top of the band.

### Pump J: the exponent cash register

Write every power before judging closeness:

```text
source depth:                  Y^-.001
transverse target:             Y^-.0179
upper antenna target:          Y^-.019
hypothetical FC conversion:    Y^(-41/66)=Y^-.6212...
smooth PWCT pseudo-model:      Y^-.499.              (6.6)
```

Do not use percentages such as “90% solved” for theorem distance.  An
unproved fixed-power bridge is binary, even if many surrounding lemmas are
known.

### Pump K: the adapter socket

Outputs must type-check against the next theorem.  Preserve

```text
actual-prime mask;
source normalization;
coefficient sign/cone;
global one-sided floor;
full height band;
B^-1 precision;
strict exponent reserve.                             (6.7)
```

PWCT controls only nonpositive separators.  Signed `DPA_P` does not supply a
positive carrier antenna.  Downstream reward remains zero until the complete
inequality chain is displayed.

### Pump L: remove the ornament

If a proposed mechanism lives on a small special sector, delete that sector
and charge the loss.  Pair deletion costs only `Y^(-17/33+o(1))`, much less
than the legal source mass `Y^-.001`.  This says the pairs do not carry the
bulk source negativity.  It does **not** preserve contiguity of the residual
event and does not prevent pair coordinates from cancelling high-band peaks.
Any stronger conclusion needs a separate theorem.

### Pump M: the local-to-global tax

Keep packet count, overlap, remainder, and allocation weights in every
displayed inequality.  A local gain is not global until its summation norm is
controlled.  Phrases such as “sum over packets” and “choose a good scale” are
red flags when the corresponding factor disappears.

### Pump N: first-open-edge quotienting

Canonicalize every proof sketch by its first unproved edge.  Twenty stories
whose first open edge is LTRAD are one research route, not twenty pieces of
evidence.  Duals and equivalent SDPs remain in the same equivalence class.

### Pump O: finite microscope, not telescope

Finite computation may answer:

```text
Which constraints are active?
Does the optimizer avoid the source?
Does a cap bind?
How many contacts appear?
Does an actual/control difference have a stable sign?
```

It may not answer an asymptotic exponent without a proved scaling theorem.
Record bracket orientation, numerical trust, event legality, and exactly
what each control preserves.

### Pump P: sufficient is not necessary

Whenever a restricted theorem is proposed, draw both arrows.  For example,

```text
diffuse nonnegative PDPA_+
  -> delta_+^C<=Y^(-.019+o(1)),

PDPA_+ + PWCT (eta<10^-4)
  -> no legal event,
```

but no converse or adapter from signed `DPA_P` is known.  Refuting diffuse
`PDPA_+` does not close non-diffuse positive antennas or signed `DPA_P`.

### Pump Q: the milestone ladder

At the end of every argument ask:

```text
Did we prove a local lemma?
Did we close one strip input?
Did we prove the strip?
Did we build strip-to-RH amplification?
Did we prove RH?
```

Never skip a rung in prose.
