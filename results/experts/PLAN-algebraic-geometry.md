## 1. Reformulation

### 1.1 The central objects, in my field's language

The program's object is λ(L) = inf{ Q_L(φ) : ‖φ‖₂ = 1, supp φ ⊂ [−L/4, L/4] },
where Q_L = pole + archimedean − primes is the truncated Weil form (`THEOREMS.md`
header), equal under RH to 2Σ_{γ>0}|φ̂(γ)|² — the lower frame bound of the
exponential system at the zero ordinates. The measured law is
ln λ(L) ≈ 10.2 − 1.755·e^{L/2}(L/2 + 4.0), smooth through prime thresholds
(glide proved, `THEOREMS.md` Theorem 1), universal over real Dirichlet characters
in the family Nyquist height T*_χ = (2π/q_χ)e^{L/2}, and a functional of the
counting function N(T) alone (smooth-staircase experiment, `RESULTS.md` day two,
fourth session).

In algebraic geometry this entire structure has a world where it is a **theorem**:
a smooth projective geometrically connected curve C of genus g over F_q. There

  Z(C, T) = P_C(T) / ((1−T)(1−qT)),  P_C(T) = ∏_{j=1}^{2g} (1 − α_j T),

and RH for C is |α_j| = √q for all j (Hasse 1936 for g = 1; Weil 1948 in general:
*Sur les courbes algébriques et les variétés qui s'en déduisent*; *Variétés
abéliennes et courbes algébriques*). Write α_j = √q·e^{iθ_j}. The zeros of
ζ_C(s) = Z(C, q^{−s}) are

  Γ_C = { γ = (θ_j + 2πk)/ln q : j = 1..2g, k ∈ ℤ },

a **periodic** ordinate set: 2g zeros per period 2π/ln q, constant density
D_C = g·ln q/π. The Weil explicit formula for C is exact and finite (the
formalism as used below is Serre's: *Zeta and L functions*, in Arithmetical
Algebraic Geometry, Purdue 1963, Harper & Row 1965; the positivity viewpoint on
the Weil conjectures is also Serre, *Analogues kählériens de certaines
conjectures de Weil*, Ann. of Math. 71, 1960); the
positivity that proves RH is the Castelnuovo–Severi inequality / Hodge index
theorem on the surface C × C (Weil 1948; Mattuck–Tate, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg
1958; Grothendieck, *Sur une note de Mattuck–Tate*, J. reine angew. Math. 200,
1958), equivalently positivity of the Rosati involution's trace form on
End(Jac C) ⊗ ℝ. Grothendieck's Lefschetz formula (Sém. Bourbaki 279, 1964/65)
realizes the zeros as eigenvalues of Frobenius F on H¹_ét(C̄, ℚ_ℓ), dim = 2g;
Deligne's purity (Weil I, Publ. IHÉS 43, 1974; Weil II, Publ. IHÉS 52, 1980)
is the weights language used below.

The exact curve-side ledger (derived by Poisson summation over the cosets of
Γ_C; proof inside Lemma AG-1): for real φ supported in [−L/4, L/4],

  Q_{C,L}(φ) := Σ_{γ ∈ Γ_C} |φ̂(γ)|²
             = ln q · [ 2g·ψ_φ(0) + 2 Σ_{1 ≤ n ≤ L/(2 ln q)} τ_n · ψ_φ(n ln q) ],
  τ_n = Σ_j cos(nθ_j) = (q^n + 1 − #C(F_{q^n}))/q^{n/2}   ( = arch + pole − primes ),

with |τ_n| ≤ 2g the Hasse–Weil bound — **the ledger inequality of §2.10 is,
verbatim, the Weil bound**. Define λ_C(L) = inf over unit φ. This is the exact
transplant of the repo's λ(L), computable by `src/model_zeros.py`'s frame
instrument with gammas = Γ_C truncated.

### 1.3 The three degenerations that make the curve case solvable

(i) **Constant density ⇒ a wall.** The window's Nyquist demand is density
(L/2)/2π; the curve supplies g ln q/π forever. For L/2 < 2·(#distinct angles)·ln q
the system is an (over)complete frame; beyond, it is incomplete and λ_C = 0
exactly. ζ's density grows without bound, so its wall sits at L = ∞: **UPT is
the statement that the approach to an infinitely distant wall is controlled.**

(ii) **Riemann–Roch terminates the fluctuations.** For deg > 2g − 2 the count of
effective divisors is exactly h·(q^{deg−g+1} − 1)/(q − 1): beyond the canonical
degree there is no arithmetic left, only the pole. That finiteness ⇔ dim H¹ = 2g
⇔ the kernel closes ⇔ margins vanish identically past the wall. ζ's margins are
(under RH) strictly positive at every L — "Spec ℤ has infinite genus," and the
measured envelope is the quantitative form of that statement.

(iii) **No archimedean place ⇒ no glide.** `THEOREMS.md` Theorem 1's continuity
modulus runs entirely on Lemma A's log-growth of Re ψ(1/4 + ir/2) (the
non-concentration Lemmas B–E). The curve's archimedean weight is flat; the
corresponding margin is a **staircase with genuine downward jumps at every
threshold** (Lemma AG-1(c), verified numerically here). The repo's proved glide
is therefore identified as an *archimedean-place phenomenon*: the digamma is
what smooths ζ's staircase.

### 1.4 The pullback obstruction: precisely what fails over ℚ

The function-field proof consumes four ingredients; here is each with its
ℚ-status and claimed replacement:

1. **The self-product C ×_{F_q} C.** Positivity is intersection theory on one
   surface. Over ℚ the base F_q is gone; "Spec ℤ ×_{F₁} Spec ℤ" does not exist
   classically. Claimed home: Connes–Consani's arithmetic site (C. R. Acad. Sci.
   Paris 352, 2014) and scaling site (Selecta Math. 23, 2017), whose points
   realize the adele class space ℚ^×\A_ℚ/Ẑ^× and whose "Frobenius" is the
   scaling flow; their Riemann–Roch strategy on the square of the scaling site
   (2019–2023 series) is the explicit attempt to rebuild ingredient 1.
2. **Hodge index / Castelnuovo–Severi on that surface** (equivalently Rosati
   positivity). Over ℚ: open. Partial replacement: Connes–Consani, *Weil
   positivity and trace formula: the archimedean place* (Selecta Math. 27, 2021,
   no. 77) prove a positivity capturing the pole + archimedean landscape via the
   Sonin space of the prolate operator (continued in Connes–Consani–Moscovici,
   arXiv:2310.18423) — the archimedean third of Castelnuovo, with the primes
   outstanding. The repo's measured envelope constants b ≈ 1.755, offset 4.0
   live exactly in this Sonin/prolate territory (`ENVELOPE.md`, sharpest
   question).
3. **Cayley–Hamilton (dim H¹ = 2g < ∞).** This is what makes function-field
   uniformity *free*: one finite matrix bounds all thresholds at once (Lemma
   AG-2(iii)). Over ℚ the cascade never terminates — the repo has measured its
   Weyl staircase — so uniformity must instead be a growth inequality along an
   infinite tower (PROGRAM.md Addendum's Golod–Shafarevich-shaped question is
   the right shape).
4. **Discrete value group q^ℤ.** All curve thresholds are commensurable
   (multiples of ln q), which yields the exact Zak/fiber reduction of Lemma AG-1
   — the tool that trivializes the computation. ζ's thresholds {log p} are
   ℚ-linearly independent: no fiber reduction exists, and the frame problem is
   genuinely infinite-dimensional. This is a *fifth*, rarely stated obstruction,
   and it is why the curve case is linear algebra while ζ is analysis.

## 2. Lemma candidates

### Lemma AG-1 (Exact staircase law: the function-field margin is a Toeplitz minimum)

**(a) Statement.** Let C/F_q be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve
of genus g ≥ 1 with Frobenius angle multiset Θ = {θ_1, …, θ_2g} ⊂ (−π, π]
(conjugation-symmetric), μ_C = Σ_j δ_{θ_j}, τ_k = Σ_j cos(kθ_j) ∈ q^{−k/2}ℤ,
and let r = #{distinct values in Θ}. For L > 0 define Q_{C,L} and λ_C(L) as in
§1.2 on H_L = L²[−L/4, L/4]. Let T_n(μ_C) = [τ_{m−m'}]_{m,m'=1..n} (τ_0 = 2g).
Then, for every L > 0 with L/(2 ln q) ∉ ℤ, and with n(L) = ⌊L/(2 ln q)⌋ + 1:

  (identity)   Q_{C,L}(φ) = Σ_{γ ∈ Γ_C} |φ̂(γ)|²  for all φ ∈ H_L;
  (reduction)  λ_C(L) = ln q · λ_min( T_{n(L)}(μ_C) );
  (staircase)  λ_C is piecewise constant, non-increasing, with downward jumps
               exactly at the thresholds L = 2n ln q, of size
               ln q·[λ_min(T_n) − λ_min(T_{n+1})] ≥ 0 (Cauchy interlacing);
               at thresholds λ_C is left-continuous;
  (wall)       λ_C(L) > 0 ⟺ n(L) ≤ r; in particular λ_C ≡ 0 for L > 2r·ln q.
               Generic case (r = 2g): wall at L_crit = 4g·ln q; each angle
               collision (multiplicity) lowers the wall by 2 ln q;
  (criterion)  the following are equivalent: (1) all |α_j| = √q (RH for C);
               (2) λ_C(L) ≥ 0 for all L; (3) (τ_k)_{k ≥ 0} is a positive-definite
               sequence (Carathéodory–Toeplitz/Herglotz). If (1) fails then
               |τ_k| → ∞ ⊃ τ_0, so some explicit finite T_n has a negative
               eigenvalue: RH_C-falsity has a finite integer certificate —
               the same Π₁ asymmetry the program runs on.

**(b) Proof strategy.** (identity): Poisson summation on each coset
{(θ_j + 2πk)/ln q}: Σ_k h(θ_j + 2πk) = (1/2π)Σ_n ĥ(n)e^{inθ_j} with
h(θ) = |φ̂(θ/ln q)|², ĥ(n) = 2π ln q·ψ_φ(n ln q) (Wiener–Khinchin); sum over j
and insert Σ_j e^{inθ_j} = τ_n; the pole/arch/prime regrouping is the Lefschetz
count #C(F_{q^n}) = q^n + 1 − Σα_j^n. (reduction): the frame operator
(Sφ)(x) = ln q·Σ_m τ_m·φ(x − m ln q)·1_window is block-diagonalized by the
fibers x₀ + (ln q)ℤ ∩ window; each fiber of cardinality k carries the matrix
ln q·T_k(μ_C); cardinalities are n(L) and n(L) − 1; interlacing picks n(L).
(wall): rank T_n(μ) = min(n, r) since T_n = V*WV with V the r×n Vandermonde at
the distinct angles, W = multiplicity weights. (criterion): ⇐ Herglotz;
⇒ boundedness of PD sequences; the positivity itself is Weil's theorem — see
AG-2. (staircase): monotonicity re-proves the repo's Theorem 1 Step 1 verbatim
(zero-extension); the jumps witness that Theorem 1's *continuity* genuinely
needs its archimedean Lemma A.

**(c) Hardest missing step.** None deep: careful treatment of threshold points
L = 2n ln q (fiber cardinality bookkeeping) and of multiplicities (the frame
form scales repeated atoms, it never adds derivative-sampling directions — worth
stating precisely because supersingular curves realize it). This is a
theorem-with-complete-proof-sketch, ready to be written.

**(d) Difficulty.** Days (write-up + repo-grade verification).

**(e) Numerical stress-test (run first; instruments adapt in an afternoon).**
Feed `src/model_zeros.py::frame_form` (or a 40-line clone) the periodized set
Γ_C ∩ [−Γ, Γ] and scan L across thresholds; compare with ln q·λ_min(T_n) from
the integer point counts. Concrete regression targets, from the two

  predicted rungs ln q·λ_min(T_n) = 3.218876 (n = 1), **1.059588** (n = 2,
  = (2 − 3/√5)ln 5), 0 (n = 3; wall at L = 4 ln 5 ≈ 6.4378).

the basis manufactures a glide. Predicted sharpening with m is itself a test.

### Lemma AG-2 (Function-field UPT with explicit constants; uniformity = finite-dimensionality)

**(a) Statement.** With notation of AG-1, x_c := Σ_{m=1}^{n} c_m q^{−m/2} F^m
∈ End(Jac C) ⊗ ℝ for c ∈ ℝⁿ. Then:

  (i) [positivity, with provenance] cᵀ T_n(μ_C) c = Tr( x_c · x_c† | H¹ ) ≥ 0
      for all n, c, where † is the Rosati involution of a polarization and
      F†F = q. Positivity is the Castelnuovo–Severi inequality / Hodge index on
      C × C (Weil 1948; Mattuck–Tate 1958; Grothendieck 1958). Equality for
      c ≠ 0 first occurs at n = 2g + 1 with c = the coefficient vector of
      ∏_j (z − e^{iθ_j}) — i.e. of the normalized L-polynomial
      q^{−g}P_C(√q·z)|_{reversed} — by Cayley–Hamilton. *The kernel is the zeta
      function*: the exact statement of which the repo's keyhole (§2.13,
      validated against Groskin arXiv:2605.20224) is the ℚ-shadow.
  (ii) [explicit uniform lower bound] If the angles are distinct with circular
      separation δ = min_{i≠j} dist_{S¹}(θ_i, θ_j) > 0, then for all n ≤ 2g:
        λ_min(T_n(μ_C)) ≥ ( sin(δ/2) )^{2(n−1)} / n²
      (route: T_n(μ_C) ⪰ V_n*V_n over any n chosen atoms, V_n the n×n node
      matrix [e^{imθ_{i_l}}]; Lagrange interpolation gives
      ‖V_n^{−1}‖_∞ ≤ max_i ∏_{j≠i} 2/|e^{iθ_i} − e^{iθ_j}| ≤ (sin(δ/2))^{−(n−1)}
      since δ-separated unit-circle nodes satisfy |z_i − z_j| ≥ 2 sin(δ/2);
      then σ_min ≥ 1/(n·‖V_n^{−1}‖_∞)). Hence
        λ_C(L) ≥ ln q · (sin(δ/2))^{2(n(L)−1)} / n(L)²  for all L < L_crit:
      a *fully explicit function-field UPT*, with the envelope's decay constant
      = 2·ln(1/angle separation) per threshold.
  (iii) [what uniformity is] The §3 template's normalization N_p is the identity
      and the uniform constant is the single number
        c_C := min_{n ≤ 2g} λ_min(T_n(μ_C)) = λ_min(T_{2g}(μ_C)) > 0
      (monotone by interlacing). There is no per-prime induction anywhere in
      the proof: one geometric inequality on one surface bounds every threshold
      simultaneously, and the uniformity is exactly dim H¹ < ∞.
  (iv) [genus 1 in closed form] For elliptic E/F_q with trace a:
      λ_C(L)/ln q = 2 for L ≤ 2 ln q; = 2 − |a|/√q for 2 ln q < L ≤ 4 ln q;
      = 0 beyond. Moreover (2 − |a|/√q)(2 + |a|/√q) = (4q − a²)/q =
      |disc(x² − ax + q)|/q: **the mid-ladder margin is the Frobenius
      discriminant**, i.e. the Hasse-bound slack; the wall value degenerates
      exactly for the supersingular a = ±2√q over F_{p²}.

**(b) Proof strategy.** (i) is the classical chain, to be written in the repo's
normalization: correspondences on C × C mod trivial ≅ End(Jac), σ(x∘x') = trace
form, Castelnuovo ⇒ Rosati-positivity ⇒ the displayed trace identity by
(F^m)† = q^m F^{−m} and Tr(F^k|H¹) = q^{k/2}τ_k. (ii) is elementary matrix
analysis over the Vandermonde factorization; (iii) is interlacing plus (i);
(iv) is a 2×2/3×3 computation (verified numerically in AG-1(e)).

**(c) Hardest missing step.** For (ii), closing the gap between the
interpolation bound (exponential in n with rate 2 ln(1/δ)) and the true
Christoffel-function asymptotics of atomic measures (Grenander–Szegő theory) —
needed if one wants the *sharp* function-field envelope, not just a uniform
floor. For (i), nothing is missing mathematically; the work is writing it in
the certified-ledger normalization so the repo's oracles can consume it.

**(d) Difficulty.** (i)+(iii)+(iv): days. (ii) sharp form: weeks.

**(e) Numerical stress-test.** All T_n data are algebraic integers after
scaling: q^{k/2}τ_k ∈ ℤ. So: (1) exact-arithmetic certification — scale
T_n by q^{(n−1)/2}-diagonals to a matrix over ℤ[√q], run integer Cholesky/char-
poly, and produce *kernel-checkable* positivity certificates for named curves;
this is the function-field rung 0 of Track A's Curry–Howard ladder and is
orders of magnitude lighter than the ζ Bridge Proposition (no digamma, no
Bernoulli tails — pure integer linear algebra; a Lean artifact in a weekend,
same certificate format as `lean/weilcert`). (2) Test (ii)'s bound against
truth: for 100 random genus-2/3 curves, plot λ_min(T_n) vs (sin(δ/2)/2)^{2(n−1)}
— measure how lossy the Turán/interpolation route is; the loss factor is the
function-field measurement of the "certificate complexity" Track B mines.
(3) Verify (iv) against the frame instrument (done for E/F_5 [verified here]).

### Lemma AG-3 (The envelope constants are Landau–Widom data of N(T); the curve is the exactly-solved degenerate case, and a solvable ladder climbs back to ζ)

**(a) Statement (two provable parts and one identified analytic target).**

  (i) [rigid limit = zero toll] For the maximally rigid configuration —
      equidistributed angles θ_j = π(2j − g − 1)/g·(any rotation), i.e. μ =
      the 2g-th-roots lattice — τ_k = 2g·1_{2g | k}, so T_n = 2g·I for n ≤ 2g:
      λ_C(L) = 2g·ln q, *flat at the ceiling all the way to the wall*, then 0.
      Among all angle configurations at fixed (g, q), the perfectly rigid one
      maximizes every rung (T_n(μ) ⪰ ... via majorization of the fiber symbols).
      This is the exact curve-side form of the repo's §2.17 finding that the
      true zeros sit at the maximally-rigid offset and that rigidity enters
      only the offset: *for a genuinely periodic (lattice) spectrum the toll is
      zero; all decay comes from deviation-from-lattice.*
  (ii) [solvable ladder toward ζ] For a "spliced" frequency set — union of two
      periodic sets with incommensurable-in-pairs spacings on two height bands
      (the simplest model of a *drifting* density) — the same Zak reduction
      gives quasi-periodic fiber operators (finite band Toeplitz-block form),
      and λ(L) is computable to all orders; iterating splices approximates the
      Riemann–von Mangoldt density (1/2π)ln(T/2π). Statement: the spliced
      models' margins interpolate between the curve staircase and the measured
      ζ envelope, with per-band tolls given by the band's local
      spacing-to-window ratio.
  (iii) [the identified target] The measured ζ exponent decomposes as
        1.755·e^{L/2}(L/2 + 4) = ∫₀^{T*(L)} 1.755·( ln(T/2π) + 5 ) dT/2π,
      i.e. per-pair toll t(T) = 1.755·(1 + 5/ln(T/2π)) per zero pair at height
      T < T*(L) (asymptotically constant 1.755 per pair, enhanced ×3 at the
      measured heights — which is exactly why `RESULTS.md`'s constant-toll fit
      misses by 20×). Conjecture to prove (M3-adjacent): t(T) is the
      Landau–Widom/Christoffel toll of the RvM density, derivable from the
      extremal problem "minimize Σ w(γ)|φ̂(γ)|² over PW(L/4)" with drifting
      lattice nodes, and b = 1.755, offset 4.0 are *density functionals with no
      arithmetic content* — the analytic statement of the repo's
      smooth-staircase experiment (`src/model_zeros.py`: smooth ≈ true at
      2.75 vs 2.69 e−10 (L = 2.485), 3.18 vs 2.71 e−15 (L = 2.996); Poisson
      1.5–2 orders worse).

**(b) Proof strategy.** (i): direct computation + a majorization argument over
fiber symbols; (ii): the AG-1 Zak method with two spacings — the fiber operator
becomes an almost-Mathieu-flavored quasi-periodic band matrix; for *rationally*

danger is concrete: an elegant transplant that validates instruments, decorates
the dictionary, and transfers zero leverage to ℚ — algebraic geometry's
recurring sin in this subject since 1948.

Mitigations, honestly weighed. First, the negative results are themselves
load-bearing: identifying the glide as archimedean (3) and uniformity as
finite-dimensionality (4) tells the ℚ program *exactly which two miracles it

### (b) AG-1 made paper-ready: the full proof sketch

Conventions: C/F_q smooth projective geometrically connected, genus g ≥ 1;
angles Θ = {θ_1,…,θ_2g} (conjugation-symmetric multiset), r = #distinct;
μ_C = Σ_j δ_{θ_j}; τ_k = Σ_j cos kθ_j = S_k/q^{k/2}, S_k = q^k + 1 − #C(F_{q^k})
∈ ℤ; W = L/2; window I = [0, W) (translation of [−L/4, L/4]; the frame operator
conjugates by a modulation, so all spectra below are translation-invariant);
Γ_C = {(θ_j + 2πk)/ln q}; φ̂(γ) = ∫_I φ e^{−iγx} dx.

**Step 1 (a priori convergence).** For any 1-separated Γ and φ ∈ L²(I),
Σ_{γ∈Γ}|φ̂(γ)|² ≤ C(W)‖φ‖² (Plancherel–Pólya / Ingham upper frame bound;
Γ_C is a finite union of separated cosets). So Q_{C,L}(φ) := Σ_{Γ_C}|φ̂(γ)|²
is a bounded quadratic form and λ_C(L) := inf_{‖φ‖=1} Q_{C,L}(φ) is finite.

**Step 2 (the Parseval–Zak identity — the core, distribution-free).** Write
p = ln q. For x₀ ∈ [0, p) let k(x₀) = #{m ≥ 0 : x₀ + mp < W} ∈ {n−1, n},
n = n(L) := ⌈W/p⌉, and φ_m(x₀) := φ(x₀ + mp). For a single coset
γ_k = (θ + 2πk)/p, substitute x = x₀ + mp:

  φ̂(γ_k) = ∫_0^p [ Σ_m φ_m(x₀) e^{−iθm} ] e^{−i(θ+2πk)x₀/p} dx₀,

and since {e^{−2πikx₀/p}}_{k∈ℤ} is an orthogonal basis of L²[0,p) with norms²
= p, Parseval in k gives (the extra unimodular factor e^{−iθx₀/p} drops out):

  Σ_{k∈ℤ} |φ̂((θ+2πk)/p)|² = p ∫_0^p | Σ_m φ_m(x₀) e^{−iθm} |² dx₀.

Sum over θ = θ_j, j = 1..2g, and expand the square:
Σ_j |Σ_m c_m e^{−iθ_j m}|² = Σ_{m,m'} c_m c̄_{m'} τ_{m−m'} (τ real by
conjugation symmetry). Hence, exactly:

  Q_{C,L}(φ) = ln q · ∫_0^{p} ⟨ T_{k(x₀)}(μ_C) φ_•(x₀), φ_•(x₀) ⟩ dx₀.   (★)

No Poisson summation of distributions, no interchange issues: two applications
of Fubini (justified by Step 1) and scalar Parseval.

**Step 3 (the ledger form and the dictionary).** Expanding (★) in the diagonal
n′ = m − m′ and integrating in x₀ reassembles the autocorrelation:
Q_{C,L}(φ) = ln q [2g·ψ_φ(0) + 2Σ_{n′≥1} τ_{n′} ψ_φ(n′ ln q)] (finite sum:
ψ_φ(u) = 0 for u ≥ W). Inserting τ_{n′} = q^{n′/2} + q^{−n′/2} − N_{n′}q^{−n′/2}
(Grothendieck–Lefschetz: N_k = q^k + 1 − Σ_j α_j^k) and
N_k = Σ_{d|k} d·a_d (a_d = # closed points of degree d) yields the
pole/archimedean/prime ledger of §1.2 with Λ_C(v^m) = log Nv — the exact
transplant of the repo's certified §6 normalization. (This step is bookkeeping;
it is where the NT seat should check factors against `src/oracle.py`

**Step 4 (reduction to the Toeplitz minimum).** (★) exhibits the frame
operator as a direct integral over x₀ ∈ [0, p) of the matrices
ln q·T_{k(x₀)}(μ_C). Therefore λ_C(L) = ln q · ess-inf_{x₀} λ_min(T_{k(x₀)}).
The fibers with k = n form a set of positive measure whenever W/p ∉ ℤ (measure
W − (n−1)p), and T_{n−1} is a principal submatrix of T_n, so Cauchy interlacing
gives λ_min(T_{n−1}) ≥ λ_min(T_n) and

  λ_C(L) = ln q · λ_min(T_{n(L)}(μ_C)),  n(L) = ⌊L/(2 ln q)⌋ + 1  (L/(2 ln q) ∉ ℤ).

The infimum is realized in the limit by unit φ concentrated on the deepest
fibers (measurable selection is trivial here: T is constant in x₀ on each of
