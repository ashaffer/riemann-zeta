# QP self-orbit log holonomy and the row-span triangle dichotomy

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Verdict:** every rounded self-orbit edge carries an integer cocycle which
is within `D^2/(16q)(1+o(1))` of an exact logarithmic gradient.  Therefore
all cycles of length

```text
m D^2 < 16 q (1-D/q^2)
```

have exactly zero cocycle circulation.  In particular, `D^2<q` forces every
cycle through length 16 to be flat.

For triangles this yields a sharp structural dichotomy.  If the three
carrier rows span less than `e^(-0.4)q/D`, the three physical self-orbit
vertices are exactly affine-collinear, hence seed an already recognized
coherent packet.  This does **not** classify every triangle: a critical
full-integer fixture has a noncollinear zero-holonomy triangle with a row
jump `8341`, well above the `q/D` threshold.  Thus the new result isolates
the remaining obstruction as row-separated/nonreturn holonomy; it does not
prove the total-edge theorem or the sharp four-cycle bound.

## 1. The exact logarithmic gradient

Let `v_i=(b_i,B_i)` and suppose the oriented edge `i -> j` has actual row
`x=x_ij` satisfying

```text
8 x b_i B_j=q^3+r,       8 x B_i b_j=q^3+s,
|r|,|s|<=qD.                                           (1.1)
```

Put

```text
Q=q^3/8,
w_ij=x det(v_i,v_j)=(r-s)/8 in Z,
F_i=Q log(b_i/B_i).                                   (1.2)
```

With `u=r/q^3`, `v=s/q^3`, and `phi(z)=log(1+z)-z`, there is the exact
identity

```text
(F_i-F_j)-w_ij=Q(phi(u)-phi(v)).                     (1.3)
```

Write `a=D/q^2`.  On `[-a,a]`, `phi` has maximum zero at zero and minimum
at `-a`.  Indeed

```text
[-log(1-a)-a]-[a-log(1+a)]
 =log((1+a)/(1-a))-2a >=0.                           (1.4)
```

Consequently the range, rather than the sum of two absolute Taylor
remainders, gives

```text
|phi(u)-phi(v)|
 <=-log(1-a)-a
 <=a^2/[2(1-a)].                                     (1.5)
```

Thus the uniform per-edge error is

```text
|(F_i-F_j)-w_ij|
 <= D^2/[16q(1-D/q^2)].                              (1.6)
```

No shell constants enter (1.6); they enter only in the triangle conversion
below.  The factor 16 is valid because (1.5) bounds the range of one
one-variable function.  A separate triangle inequality for the two Taylor
remainders would give the weaker, unnecessary factor 8.

## 2. Exact short-cycle flatness

The `F` terms telescope around every oriented cycle, while every `w_ij` is
an integer.  Hence a cycle of length `m` obeys

```text
|sum_cycle w_ij|
 <=m D^2/[16q(1-D/q^2)].                             (2.1)
```

If the right side is below one, the integer circulation is zero.  This
proves the displayed threshold in the verdict.  Moreover, for integral
`q,D` with `D^2<q`,

```text
D^2/q < 1-D/q^2,                                    (2.2)
```

because `q(q-D^2)>=q>D`.  Therefore the per-edge bound in (1.6) is strictly
below `1/16`, proving flatness through length 16.  At
`D=q^(16/33+o(1))`, the guaranteed length is of order
`q/D^2=q^(1/33+o(1))`.

This is a local theorem.  It produces an integer potential on any component
whose full cycle space is generated by cycles below the threshold.  It does
not rule out a long-holonomy skeleton, and it does not count the potentially
many edges determined by such a skeleton.

## 3. A triangle is a row plane

For an oriented triangle write

```text
kappa_12=det(v_1,v_2),
kappa_23=det(v_2,v_3),
kappa_31=det(v_3,v_1).                               (3.1)
```

Zero circulation is

```text
x_12 kappa_12+x_23 kappa_23+x_31 kappa_31=0,         (3.2)
```

which is exactly

```text
det [ b_1 B_1 x_23
      b_2 B_2 x_31
      b_3 B_3 x_12 ]=0.                             (3.3)
```

If the vertex directions are nonparallel, there is therefore a rational
linear functional `L` with

```text
x_23=L(v_1),       x_31=L(v_2),       x_12=L(v_3).  (3.4)
```

For one triangle this plane law is equivalent to holonomy and has two free
rational coefficients.  It is not by itself an affine/Hankel packet
classification.  Its leverage appears only after using the row span.

## 4. The exact row-span dichotomy

The integral affine area is

```text
A=det(v_2-v_1,v_3-v_1)
 =kappa_12+kappa_23+kappa_31.                        (4.1)
```

Let `x_*` be the median of the three rows, `H=max x-min x`, and
`K=max |kappa_ij|`.  Subtracting `x_*` times (4.1) from (3.2) gives, exactly,

```text
x_* |A| <= H K.                                     (4.2)
```

The two edge windows also give

```text
|x_ij kappa_ij|<=qD/4.                              (4.3)
```

In the project shell, every row is at least

```text
x_min=(q/2)e^(-0.2),
```

so `K<=qD/(4x_min)`.  Combining this with (4.2), the sufficient condition

```text
H < 4x_min^2/(qD)=e^(-0.4)q/D                      (4.4)
```

makes `|A|<1`.  Since `A` is integral, `A=0`: the three physical orbit
vertices are exactly collinear.  Such a triangle is a legitimate coherent
packet seed, although charging all overlapping seeds to merged packets is
a separate global step.

Conversely, every noncollinear zero-holonomy triangle must have a row jump
at least the scale in (4.4).  This is the precise survivor left by the
triangle law.

## 5. Hostile and actual-prime replays

The critical full-integer fixture

```text
q=100000,       D=265,
v_1=(50008,50001),
v_2=(50002,49995),
v_3=(42858,42852),
(x_12,x_23,x_31)=(49997,58338,58331)                (5.1)
```

has

```text
(kappa_12,kappa_23,kappa_31)=(-42,-6,42),
(w_12,w_23,w_31)=(-2099874,-350028,2449902),
sum w=0,
A=-6,
H=8341,
L(b,B)=b/2+2B/3.                                    (5.2)
```

All six literal windows hold.  This is the nonreturn part of the previously
recorded induced bipartite `C6`: after self-orbit reflection that `C6`
collapses to two triangles sharing an edge.  Its unweighted alternating row
sum of one is a different invariant and does not contradict (2.1).

The seven-prime critical fixture behaves oppositely.  Its symmetric graph
has five vertices and seven triangles.  Every one has zero circulation,
affine area zero, and row span at most `36`; all seven are therefore exact
collinear packet seeds, far inside the `e^(-0.4)q/D` threshold.
