## Novelty firewall

Before describing a mathematical claim as new, frontier, or a breakthrough:

1. search the local theorem registry and reports for both its formula and its
   structural analogue;
2. compare the exact hypotheses and conclusion with primary literature,
   including current preprints, and record the closest theorem-level overlap;
   preserve a dated search-log artifact listing databases, representative
   queries, stable identifiers, versions, and access dates;
3. label the claim as `IMPORTED`, `LOCAL_PREDECESSOR`, `PROJECT_SYNTHESIS`,
   `NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED`, or `CANDIDATE_NEW`;
4. reserve `CANDIDATE_NEW` for a written theorem whose novelty delta survives
   hostile counterexamples and direct comparison with every located prior;
5. never treat failure to find a public human or AI match as proof of absence;
   private, unpublished, unindexed, and differently phrased work remain
   outside the search; and
6. do not promote `CANDIDATE_NEW` to a certified literature novelty without
   independent specialist review.

Immediate corollaries, changes of coordinate, standard functional-analytic
packaging, and elementary local algebra remain valuable, but must be called
what they are.  Every later correction must be propagated to the originating
report, theorem passport, registry entry, and replay tests.

This protocol does not apply to unrelated repository maintenance except that
existing user changes must still be preserved.
